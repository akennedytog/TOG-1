The Before: A Familiar Mess

Like most small service businesses, Coastal HVAC had grown organically. Their financial systems... hadn't.

  • Invoices sent 2-3 weeks after job completion
  • Receipts stuffed in shoeboxes (literally)
  • Monthly reports available... never
  • Owner Mark had no idea which jobs were profitable
The Breaking Point: Mark discovered they'd been underbilling recurring maintenance contracts by 15% for 8 months. "I was leaving money on the table and didn't even know it."

The Solution: AI-Powered Bookkeeping

We implemented a three-layer automation system:

1. Smart Receipt Capture

Technicians snap photos of receipts. AI extracts vendor, amount, and category automatically. No more shoeboxes.

2. Automated Invoicing

Jobs marked complete in the field → invoice generated → customer notified → payment link sent. Same day, not three weeks later.

3. Real-Time Dashboard

Mark now sees:

  • Cash position updated hourly
  • Outstanding invoices by customer
  • Job profitability in real-time

The Results

BEFORE
  • Invoicing: 2-3 week delay
  • Monthly close: 3 days
  • Cash visibility: Monthly (maybe)
  • Receipt processing: Manual
  • Annual bookkeeping cost: $4,800
AFTER
  • Invoicing: Same day
  • Monthly close: 2 hours
  • Cash visibility: Real-time
  • Receipt processing: Automated
  • Annual bookkeeping cost: $1,800
Bonus win: By catching the underbilling issue, Mark recovered $8,400 in the first 90 days. The system paid for itself in month one.

The Tech Stack

No enterprise software required. We used:

  • Receipt capture: Dext (formerly Receipt Bank) — $40/month
  • Invoicing: Integrated with their existing field service software
  • Dashboard: Custom-built report pulling from their accounting system
  • AI layer: Custom automation to connect the pieces

Total monthly cost: $150. Previous system: $400/month.
