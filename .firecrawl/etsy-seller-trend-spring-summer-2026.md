For sellers, spring and summer bring natural opportunities to lean into what feels fresh, whether that’s airy materials, garden-inspired celebrations, playful accessories, or décor that brightens a space.

## Decor and style trends

### Soft Stitch Era

![A crochet floral bag and colorful crochet bucket hat](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/082b15/7862447519/inv_fullxfull.7862447519_s08xdsfx.jpg?version=0)Photos by [Plexida](https://www.etsy.com/shop/Plexida) and [Meriva Hand Knit Shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/MerivaHandKnitACCs)

Crochet, embroidery, and stitched textures are stepping into the spotlight. As AI sameness scales, Gen Z is pushing back on fast fashion, choosing pieces made by human hands—learning to crochet themselves or buying from someone who did. Sellers deliver with sun-worn fabrics, visible mending, and little details that feel intentional.

**Rising search terms:**

- Embroidered straw bags +20,000%
- Embroidered bags +77% with Gen Z\*
- Searches for embroidered jeans up double digits
- Handmade jumpers +294
- Sales for crochet clothing +36%

For sellers, this is an opportunity to lean into the beauty of the handmade. Highlight the texture, the stitching, and the small details that make each piece unique. Close-up photography, process shots, and thoughtful descriptions that share how and why something is made can reinforce its human touch. Using keywords that call out craftsmanship can also help shoppers who are intentionally searching for pieces that feel personal and real.

### Everyday Exhibits

![A colorful array of gallery images with frames in primary colors](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/8ed490/7813247656/inv_fullxfull.7813247656_29qj2f2g.jpg?version=0)Photo by [FrameLessShop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/FrameLessShop)

As collecting becomes more accessible, sellers are redefining what it means to build a personal gallery at home, blending expressive design with nostalgic nods to childhood bedrooms, early-2000s pop culture, and the beloved objects that shaped us. Curated, personal, and a little sentimental in the best way.

**Rising search terms:**

- Wall art decor +110%
  - Fan wall art +18% with Millennials\*
- Gallery prints +80%
- Wall decor mirrors +50%
- Abstract art +38%

Sellers can support this collecting mindset by helping shoppers envision how pieces live together. Lifestyle imagery, gallery wall mockups, and clear sizing guidance can make decision-making easier. Using phrases like “gallery wall set,” “curated print collection,” or “statement wall decor” may align with how shoppers are searching. Creating cohesive series or complementary pieces can also encourage repeat visits as customers continue building their own everyday exhibit.

### World of Whimsy

![A beaded frog necklace and needlepoint of a colorful bouquet](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/4c0ea1/7813049690/inv_fullxfull.7813049690_1ccuklmp.jpg?version=0)Photos by [Wildflwr Accessories](https://www.etsy.com/shop/WildflwrAccessories) and [Nelly Makes Embroidery](https://www.etsy.com/shop/NellyMakesEmbroidery)

With so much heaviness in the world, whimsy feels like a playful escape. Shoppers are embracing what delights them, no explanation needed. What began in 2025 is taking off in 2026, as people ditch curated perfection and turn to Etsy sellers for hobby kits, polka dot everything, and anything to make ordinary life feel extraordinary.

**Rising search terms:**

- Whimsical jewelry +84%
- Polka dot phone cases +835%
- Mahjong racks +228%
  - Mahjong sets +178% with Gen Z\*
- Beginner needlepoint kits +175%

Sellers can tap into this shift by embracing personality and playfulness in both product design and presentation. Bright, expressive photography and descriptive language that evokes joy—like “whimsical,” “quirky,” or “beginner friendly”—can help listings resonate. For hobby kits especially, clearly outlining what’s included and who it’s for makes it easier for curious newcomers to say yes. Giftable bundles and themed sets can further reinforce that sense of delight and discovery.

### Dear Diary

![A stack of leather journals, an old-school reading log card insert, and an array of painted charms](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/14e8a1/7862370327/inv_fullxfull.7862370327_9leqvc54.jpg?version=0)Photo by [BoivinLeather](https://www.etsy.com/shop/BoivinLeather), [belly bean cards](https://www.etsy.com/shop/bellybeancards), and [DeskNook](https://www.etsy.com/shop/DeskNook)

With no shortage of screens vying for our attention, shoppers are reclaiming rituals that can’t be swiped away. Personal keepsakes, handwritten notes, journal accessories—the analog revival is so back, as scrapbooking and documenting turn into intentional nostalgia and a way to slow the scroll. Gen Z is leading this, redefining what connection looks like.

**Rising search terms:**

- Traveler journal searches doubled
- Journal charm +395%
  - Journal charms +479% with Gen Z\*
- Leather journal covers +126%
- Memory journals +189%

For sellers, emphasizing tactile qualities and longevity can resonate here. Close-up imagery that shows paper texture, leather grain, or charm details helps bring the product to life online. Descriptions that focus on ritual—daily journaling, memory keeping, heirloom quality—can connect emotionally with shoppers seeking something lasting. Offering personalization or add-ons may further enhance that sense of ownership and meaning.

### Treat Yourself

![A customized dog bowl and box of candles with a 20-minute burn time for mindfulness](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/4bb8b8/7860963859/inv_fullxfull.7860963859_ea5yczyq.jpg?version=0)Photos by [Needs Pottery](https://www.etsy.com/shop/NeedsPottery) and [Modo Creative](https://www.etsy.com/shop/modocreative)

For Gen Z, the “little treat” era isn’t impulsive, it’s intentionally acknowledging that the smaller moments matter. New place, finished the laundry, made it through the week? It’s all worth celebrating, and sellers get it. They’re turning life’s smallest victories into custom “treats” that feel made for the moment, not the masses.

**Rising search terms:**

- Petit cadeau +277%
- 39% of Gen Z celebrated "just because" moments last year
- 55% of Gen Z bought a gift in the past year, outpacing Millennials

Sellers might consider how their products can speak to everyday celebrations, not just major milestones. Language like “little treat,” “just because,” or “thinking of you” can broaden a product’s relevance and help shoppers see it as a small but meaningful indulgence. Accessible price points, gift-ready packaging, and clear, emotive descriptions can all support spontaneous purchases rooted in joy rather than obligation.

## Wedding trends

### Botanical Bride

![Stained glass florals and a watercolor invitation set](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/8cc34c/7861171685/inv_fullxfull.7861171685_q4dp4nh5.jpg?version=0)Photos by [Alexa Workshop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/AlexaWorkshop) and [Karli Strohschein Art](https://www.etsy.com/shop/KarliStrohscheinArt)

Ballroom weddings had their run. Now Gen Z and Millennial couples are rewriting the rules, trading stuffy ceremonies for garden romance, natural settings, and slower celebrations. Sellers make it personal: pressed flowers from your proposal. Embroidered napkins featuring blooms from your venue. Every detail made-to-order, directly with the maker.

**Rising search terms:**

- Wildflower wedding signs more than doubled
- Wedding statement rings +200%
- Bridesmaid baskets +4,200%
- Floral necklaces searches +99%
  - Floral gold rings +59% with Millennials \*
  - Wedding dress cover ups +3,025% with Gen Z\*

![A colorful illustrated menu card with fruits and florals](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/04b538/7861174881/inv_fullxfull.7861174881_qcx3jigm.jpg?version=0)Photo by [Lunasa Studio](https://www.etsy.com/shop/LunasaStudio)

For sellers in the wedding space, personalization and storytelling matter more than ever. Highlighting customization options and showcasing real-life use—through styled shoots or customer photos—can help couples imagine their own day. Using keyword phrases like “garden wedding,” “wildflower bride,” or “romantic bridal” may align listings with how modern couples are browsing. Emphasizing collaboration and made-to-order details reinforces the direct connection between buyer and seller, too.

### Gothic Romance

![Gothic style earrings, a lace veil, swan wedding invitation, and royal-inspired wedding invitations](https://i.etsystatic.com/inv/f0377c/7861054133/inv_fullxfull.7861054133_4w18rbtp.jpg?version=0)Photos by [Nest Pretty Things](https://www.etsy.com/shop/NestPrettyThingsShop), [Britten Weddings](https://www.etsy.com/shop/BrittenWeddings), [theeverydayandco](https://www.etsy.com/shop/theeverydayandco), and [Wish Upon Paper Studio](https://www.etsy.com/shop/WishUponPaperStudio)

Dark romance is no longer staying on the page. As readers yearn for slow-burn love stories, weddings are following suit. Cathedral lace and stained-glass details. The trick is commitment level: some want full Victorian drama, others just a lace collar for a subtle nod to the trend. Offering versions for every intensity lets shoppers decide just how moody they want to go!

**Rising search terms:**

- Mantilla veils +25%
  - Cathedral lace veils +38% with Millennials \*
- Vintage barware +1,550%
- Stained glass wedding signs +200%
- Sales for lace wedding accessories +63%

Sellers can meet this moment by offering pieces that range in intensity, allowing shoppers to choose how they lean into the aesthetic. Rich, atmospheric photography and descriptive terms like “gothic wedding,” “Victorian inspired,” or “moody decor” can help signal the vibe. Providing variations or styling suggestions may also help customers envision how to incorporate a touch—or a full embrace—of dark romance into their day or space.
