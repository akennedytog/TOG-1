# Miami

Kaufman Rossin’s main office is located in scenic Coconut Grove, at the intersection of Mary Street and Oak Avenue. Our new Miami building is just blocks from beautiful Biscayne Bay and Miami City Hall.

Kaufman Rossin is one of Florida’s largest accounting firms, offering traditional CPA services plus innovative advisory solutions to help you plan, save and grow.
