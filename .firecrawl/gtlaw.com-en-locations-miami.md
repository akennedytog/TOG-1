## Overview

As the founding office of Greenberg Traurig and the only global law firm founded in Miami, the Miami office is embedded in the business, legal, governmental, and civic fabric of South Florida. For nearly sixty years, we have helped shape the region’s evolution from a gateway to the Americas into a vibrant global center for investment and innovation. Today, we are proudly known for our “Miami Roots, Global Reach,” a recognition of both our longstanding local commitment and our role advising clients across the United States, Latin America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

With more than 200 attorneys representing the firm’s core practices, the Miami office serves clients in key industries driving South Florida’s economy, including financial services, technology, health care, real estate, hospitality, retail, construction, infrastructure, entertainment, aviation, and international finance. Our multidisciplinary team helps companies navigate complex transactions, regulatory challenges, litigation, and emerging business opportunities in one of the fastest-growing economic markets in the country.

Drawing on decades of experience in a rapidly evolving market that continues to attract new investment, our attorneys are uniquely positioned to assist clients relocating to Florida. We offer strategic, tailored guidance throughout every stage of the transition. GT remains committed to helping our clients navigate Miami, providing perspective and resources rooted in our deep connection to the region. To learn more, please visit: [**Corporate Relocation to Florida**](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/general/corporate-relocation-to-florida) and [**GT: Your Gateway to Miami**](https://www4.gtlaw.com/marketing/MIA/41727/index.html).
