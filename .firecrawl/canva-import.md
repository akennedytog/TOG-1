## Exporting your Figma files

- **Figma Design files:** we recommend uploading your individual assets into Canva as separate PNGs/ SVGs. Follow the steps found in [Uploading media from your device](https://www.canva.com/help/upload-media/).
- **FigJam files:** you can import them into Canva via PDF upload.

You can also export individual frames from Figma files and then upload these into Canva as media items.

[Copy link to heading](https://www.canva.com/help/importing-figma-files/#importing-your-figjam-files-via-pdf)

## Importing your FigJam files via PDF

Easily import your FigJam files to Canva by **any of the following** methods:

[Copy link to heading](https://www.canva.com/help/importing-figma-files/#from-the-homepage-)

### From the homepage

###### Quick tip

You can upload files directly from the homepage.

[Take me there](https://canva.me/pp-pdf-editor)

1. Drag and drop your PDF to the [homepage](https://www.canva.com/).
2. On the top corner of the homepage banner, click the **Upload** button. Drag your file to the Upload section or click on **browse**.
3. On the side menu of the homepage, click

**Projects**. Drag your file to the page to upload it, or click on the **Add new** button.

Once done, click on the imported PDF to open it in the Canva editor.

[Copy link to heading](https://www.canva.com/help/importing-figma-files/#from-the-editor)

### From the editor

1. Drag and drop your PDF to the editor.
2. On the top corner of the editor, click on **File** \>

**Upload a file**.
3. On the editor side panel, click on the

**Uploads** tab, then click on **Upload files**. Select the files you want to upload, or drag and drop them in the **Uploads** tab.

To use the file in your design, click on it from the

**Projects** tab in the editor side panel.

To find all your uploads, select

**Projects** from the homepage side panel. On the

**owner dropdown, select your name to view your projects or**

**Any owner to view all projects, then click on the**

**Uploads folder, where you can also download or delete them.**

[Copy link to heading](https://www.canva.com/help/importing-figma-files/#from-the-pdf-editor-and-converter-pages)

### From the PDF editor and converter pages

There are several pages dedicated to uploading your PDF (i.e. [PDF editor](https://www.canva.com/pdf-editor/), [PDF converter](https://www.canva.com/features/pdf-converter/)). From one of these pages, follow these steps:

1. Click on

**Upload your file** or

**Upload your PDF**.
2. Choose the file from your device.
3. Log in to your Canva account to continue.

The uploaded PDF will open in the editor where you can revise and [download it as another file type.](https://www.canva.com/help/download-or-purchase/)

You can import files of up to 100MB in size.

For more information on what to expect when uploading PDFs, see [Importing PDFs](https://www.canva.com/help/pdf-import/).
