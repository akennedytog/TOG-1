UHealth makes finding a primary care doctor easy. Whether you need an annual checkup, a flu shot, or more
comprehensive care for a chronic disease, our UHealth Primary Care physicians are here for you. We
understand medical care can sometimes get complicated, especially for patients who have chronic medical
conditions.

To schedule an appointment, please call [305-689-DOCS (3627)](tel:305-689-3627).

For immediate care of minor illness, visit [UHealthClinics.com](https://umiamihealth.org/patient-,-a-,-visitors/uhealth-clinic-at-walgreens?sc_lang=en) to make an appointment at a UHealth Clinic
at Walgreens location near you.
