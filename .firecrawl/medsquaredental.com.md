## Dr. Nicholas A. Castellanos, DDS

Dr. Nicholas A. Castellanos was born and raised in Miami and is the founder of Med Square Dental. He achieved his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Universidad Iberoamericana in 2015. Upon graduating from dental school, he successfully completed a two-year post graduate advanced training residency at Larkin Hospital where he was awarded the title of “Chief Resident”.

Dr. Castellanos is passionate about implant dentistry and has completed over 500 hours of Continuing Education in the field. He is a graduate of the prestigious American Academy of Implant Dentistry Maxi Course and has completed multiple domestic and international implant training programs.
