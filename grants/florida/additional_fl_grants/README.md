## 4. Opportunity Zones (Florida)

### Program Overview
**Type:** Federal tax incentive  
**Benefit:** Capital gains tax deferral/elimination  
**Focus:** Investment in designated zones

### Eligibility
- Investment in Opportunity Zone business
- Miami has multiple designated zones
- Long-term holding required (10 years for full benefit)

### AI Automation Company Fit
- **ELIGIBLE** if located in Opportunity Zone
- Attracts investors seeking tax benefits
- Opa-Locka, parts of Miami Gardens, others

### Resources
- **CDFI Fund:** https://www.cdfifund.gov/opportunity-zones
- **Florida Opportunity Zones Map:** Available via Enterprise Florida
