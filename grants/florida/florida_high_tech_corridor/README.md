# Florida High-Tech Corridor Matching Grant

## Program Overview
**Maximum Award:** Up to $250,000 (1:1 match required)  
**Program Type:** Research grants for industry-university collaboration  
**Focus:** High-tech sectors in Florida's 23-county Corridor region

## Eligibility Checklist

### Business Requirements
- [ ] For-profit company located in Florida
- [ ] Active business operations for at least 2 years
- [ ] Demonstrated ability to provide 1:1 cash match
- [ ] Company must be in a high-tech industry sector

### Project Requirements
- [ ] Research project involves collaboration with UCF, USF, or UF
- [ ] Project demonstrates innovation and commercialization potential
- [ ] Clear deliverables and milestones defined
- [ ] Technology has potential for job creation in Florida

### Industry Clusters (Priority Sectors)
- [ ] Advanced Manufacturing
- [ ] Aerospace & Defense
- [ ] Agriculture Technology (AgTech)
- [ ] Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning
- [ ] Biotechnology & Life Sciences
- [ ] Clean Energy
- [ ] Cybersecurity
- [ ] Financial Technology (FinTech)
- [ ] Health Technology (HealthTech)
- [ ] Information Technology
- [ ] Optics & Photonics
- [ ] Robotics

### AI Automation Company Eligibility
- [x] **ELIGIBLE** - AI/ML industry cluster
- [x] Can partner with UF, USF, or UCF

## Required Documents

1. **Grant Application Form** (available on floridahightech.com)
2. **Company Overview**
   - Business registration documents
   - Current financial statements (last 2 years)
   - Current business plan
3. **Project Proposal**
   - Technical description and objectives
   - Innovation/commercialization plan
   - Timeline with milestones
   - Budget breakdown
4. **University Collaboration Agreement**
   - Letter of support from faculty researcher
   - Research plan from university partner
   - Cost-sharing commitment from university
5. **Match Funding Documentation**
   - Proof of matching funds availability
   - Bank statements or funding commitment letters
6. **Impact Statement**
   - Job creation projections
   - Economic impact on Florida
7. **Letters of Support** (minimum 2)
   - Industry partners, customers, or community leaders

## Step-by-Step Application Guide

### Phase 1: Pre-Application (Months 1-2)
1. **Identify University Partner**
   - Contact UCF: Research Office (407) 823-5278
   - Contact USF: Office of Research & Innovation (813) 974-2897
   - Contact UF: Office of Research (352) 392-1582
   - Schedule meetings with faculty in relevant departments

2. **Develop Collaboration Agreement**
   - Draft joint research plan
   - Negotiate IP and commercialization terms
   - Secure letter of support from faculty

3. **Prepare Match Funding**
   - Secure commitments from investors or company reserves
   - Document proof of funds

### Phase 2: Application Preparation (Months 2-3)
1. Download application from floridahightech.com
2. Complete all required sections
3. Review with university partner
4. Prepare supporting documents
5. Obtain required signatures

### Phase 3: Submission (Month 3)
1. Submit complete application via online portal
2. Confirm receipt with program coordinator
3. Prepare for potential site visit/interview

### Phase 4: Award Management (If Approved)
1. Execute grant agreement
2. Set up reporting schedule
3. Begin project implementation
4. Submit quarterly progress reports
