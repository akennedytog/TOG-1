# Broward County Micro-Grant Program

## Program Overview
**Program Type:** Small business micro-grants  
**Target:** Small businesses in Broward County  
**Typical Awards:** $5,000 - $25,000  
**Important:** Opens April 27, 2026

## Eligibility Checklist

### Business Requirements
- [ ] Physically located in Broward County, Florida
- [ ] For-profit business (LLC, Corporation, Partnership, or Sole Proprietor)
- [ ] Current Broward County Business Tax Receipt (BTR)
- [ ] In operation for minimum 6-12 months (verify current requirement)
- [ ] Annual revenue under specified threshold (check current limits)
- [ ] Current on all taxes and fees
- [ ] No outstanding code violations
- [ ] Not a franchise location (may be excluded)

### Owner Requirements
- [ ] Business owner is resident of Broward County
- [ ] Owner has not received previous micro-grant (if applicable)
- [ ] Owner demonstrates financial need

### Use of Funds Requirements
- [ ] Funds used for eligible expenses only:
  - [ ] Equipment purchases
  - [ ] Technology upgrades
  - [ ] Marketing and advertising
  - [ ] Inventory
  - [ ] Professional services (accounting, legal)
  - [ ] Business training/certification
  - [ ] Renovations/improvements (if applicable)
- [ ] NOT for: debt repayment, owner salary, existing obligations

### AI Automation Company Eligibility
- [ ] **ELIGIBLE IF:** Physical presence in Broward County
- [ ] Can apply as Broward-based operation
- [ ] Technology upgrades align with eligible expenses
- [ ] Marketing to local SMBs qualifies

## Required Documents

1. **Completed Micro-Grant Application** (available April 27, 2026)
2. **Business Registration**
   - Articles of Incorporation/Organization
   - Fictitious Name Registration
   - Partnership Agreement (if applicable)
3. **Broward County Business Tax Receipt**
   - Current year BTR
   - Previous year BTR (if applicable)
4. **Tax Documentation**
   - Most recent business tax return
   - Personal tax return (if sole proprietor)
   - Quarterly filings (if applicable)
5. **Financial Statements**
   - Profit & Loss Statement (last 12 months)
   - Balance Sheet
   - Cash Flow Statement
6. **Bank Statements**
   - Last 6 months of business bank statements
7. **Business Plan**
   - Executive summary
   - Market analysis
   - Financial projections
   - Use of grant funds plan
8. **Quotes/Estimates**
   - For all items/services grant will fund
   - From licensed/registered vendors
9. **Proof of Broward Residency**
   - Owner's driver's license or utility bill
10. **Supporting Documents**
    - Lease agreement or property deed
    - Photos of business location
    - Business licenses/permits
    - Letters of recommendation (optional but helpful)

## Step-by-Step Application Guide

### Pre-Application (Now - April 2026)
1. **Confirm Eligibility**
   - Review complete guidelines when posted
   - Verify business location in Broward County
   - Confirm all licenses current

2. **Prepare Documentation**
   - Gather all business registration documents
   - Update financial statements
   - Organize tax documents
   - Take photos of business

3. **Develop Project Plan**
   - Identify specific uses for grant funds
   - Obtain vendor quotes
   - Calculate total project cost
   - Determine owner match (if required)

4. **Strengthen Application**
   - Draft business narrative
   - Prepare financial projections
   - Request letters of support
   - Review past successful applications (if available)

### Application Window (Opens April 27, 2026)
1. **Day 1: Access Application**
   - Visit broward.org/EconDev
   - Download application materials
   - Review all requirements

2. **Week 1: Complete Application**
   - Fill out all sections
   - Attach required documents
   - Double-check all information

3. **Week 2: Review and Submit**
   - Have trusted advisor review
   - Make final revisions
   - Submit before deadline
   - Keep confirmation receipt

### Post-Submission
1. Track application status
2. Respond to any requests for information
3. Prepare for potential interview
4. If approved, complete grant agreement
