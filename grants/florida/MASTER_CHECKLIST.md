## Universal Documents Needed

Gather these once, use for multiple applications:

### Business Registration
- [ ] Articles of Incorporation/Organization
- [ ] Florida Business Registration (Sunbiz)
- [ ] Federal EIN Letter
- [ ] Operating Agreement / Bylaws
- [ ] Fictitious Name Registration (if applicable)

### Financial Documents
- [ ] Last 3 years business tax returns
- [ ] Current year financial statements
  - [ ] Profit & Loss Statement
  - [ ] Balance Sheet
  - [ ] Cash Flow Statement
- [ ] Last 6 months business bank statements
- [ ] Personal financial statement (owner)
- [ ] Personal tax returns (last 2 years)

### Business Plans
- [ ] Executive summary (1-2 pages)
- [ ] Full business plan
- [ ] 3-year financial projections
- [ ] Market analysis
- [ ] Competitive analysis

### Licenses & Compliance
- [ ] Miami-Dade Business Tax Receipt (BTR)
- [ ] City of Miami Business License
- [ ] State of Florida licenses (if required)
- [ ] Certificate of good standing
- [ ] Proof of insurance

### Supporting Materials
- [ ] Owner resume/CV
- [ ] Management team bios
- [ ] Company brochure/one-pager
- [ ] Product/service descriptions
- [ ] Customer testimonials/case studies
- [ ] Photos of business location
- [ ] Professional headshots
