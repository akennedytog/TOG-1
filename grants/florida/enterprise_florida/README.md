# Enterprise Florida Incentives & Grants

## Program Overview
**Organization:** Enterprise Florida, Inc. (EFI)  
**Type:** State-level economic development incentives  
**Focus:** Business expansion, relocation, job creation  
**Target:** Businesses creating significant Florida jobs

## Eligibility Checklist

### Company Requirements
- [ ] For-profit business entity
- [ ] In good standing with Florida Department of State
- [ ] Current on all state tax obligations
- [ ] Not in default on any state or federal obligations
- [ ] Clean environmental record

### Project Requirements
- [ ] Minimum job creation thresholds (typically 10-25+ new jobs)
- [ ] Capital investment requirements (varies by program)
- [ ] Above-average wages (typically 100-150% of county average)
- - [ ] Commitment to remain in Florida for specified period

### Industry Requirements
EFI focuses on targeted industries:
- [ ] Aviation & Aerospace
- [ ] Clean Energy
- [ ] Defense & Homeland Security
- [ ] Financial & Professional Services
- [ ] Information Technology
- [ ] Life Sciences & Healthcare
- [ ] Logistics & Distribution
- [ ] Manufacturing
- [ ] Marine Sciences

### AI Automation Company Eligibility
- [x] **ELIGIBLE** - Information Technology category
- [ ] Must meet job creation minimums (typically 10-25+ jobs)
- [ ] Must pay above-average wages
- [ ] Need significant capital investment project

**Note:** Early-stage AI companies may not qualify for primary incentives due to job/wage thresholds. Consider:
- Partnering on a larger project
- Building to qualify in future funding rounds
- Exploring other Florida programs

## Available Incentive Programs

### 1. Qualified Target Industry Tax Refund (QTI)
**Value:** Up to $3,000-$6,000 per job (performance-based)  
**Requires:** Local match commitment  
**Jobs:** Minimum 10-25 new jobs  
**Wage:** 115% of average wage or higher

### 2. Capital Investment Tax Credit (CITC)
**Value:** Up to 5% of eligible capital costs  
**Requires:** Minimum $25M capital investment  
**Jobs:** Minimum 100 new jobs  
**Wage:** 150% of average wage

### 3. Quick Action Closing Fund
**Value:** Case-by-case discretionary awards  
**For:** Competitive business attraction projects  
**Use:** Infrastructure, workforce training, relocation

### 4. Incumbent Worker Training Program
**Value:** Reimbursement for training existing employees  
**Focus:** Skills upgrading for competitiveness

### 5. Small Business Development Programs
**Value:** Technical assistance, export support  
**For:** Growing small businesses

## Required Documents

### For All Programs
1. **Company Information**
   - Articles of Incorporation/Organization
   - Corporate officers and ownership structure
   - Current financial statements
   - Business plan or project summary

2. **Project Details**
   - Detailed project description
   - Timeline with milestones
   - Capital investment budget
   - Job creation projections (by position and wage)

3. **Financial Documentation**
   - Audited financials (last 2-3 years)
   - Tax returns
   - Bank references
   - Investment commitment letters

4. **Market Analysis**
   - Competitive landscape
   - Market opportunity
   - Florida advantage explanation

5. **Economic Impact Analysis**
   - Direct jobs (with wages)
   - Indirect/induced jobs
   - Tax revenue projections

6. **Local Government Support**
   - Letter from county/municipality
   - Commitment to matching funds (QTI)
   - Infrastructure support commitment

## Step-by-Step Application Guide

### Phase 1: Pre-Qualification (Months 1-2)
1. **Assess Eligibility**
   - Review EFI incentive programs online
   - Confirm job/wage thresholds align with projections
   - Verify company standing with state

2. **Engage EFI**
   - Contact regional representative
   - Schedule preliminary discussion
   - Receive program guidance

3. **Develop Project Concept**
   - Define scope and timeline
   - Estimate capital requirements
   - Project job creation
   - Identify potential locations

### Phase 2: Formal Application (Months 2-4)
1. **Prepare Documentation**
   - Compile financial statements
   - Draft detailed business plan
   - Develop economic impact analysis

2. **Secure Local Support**
   - Meet with county/municipal economic development
   - Negotiate local match commitment
   - Obtain resolution of support

3. **Submit Application**
   - Complete EFI application forms
   - Attach all required documents
   - Pay any application fees

### Phase 3: Negotiation (Months 4-6)
1. **Respond to EFI Review**
   - Provide additional information as requested
   - Participate in site visits
   - Meet with EFI leadership

2. **Negotiate Agreement**
   - Finalize incentive terms
   - Review performance requirements
   - Legal review of agreement

3. **Execute Agreement**
   - Sign incentive contract
   - Establish compliance procedures
   - Begin project implementation

### Phase 4: Compliance & Reporting (Ongoing)
1. **Track Performance**
   - Document job creation
   - Verify wage levels
   - Record capital expenditures

2. **File Reports**
   - Annual performance reports
   - Third-party verification
   - Tax filings for incentives

3. **Claim Benefits**
   - Submit for tax refunds/credits
   - Receive disbursements
   - Maintain compliance records

## Deadline Information

**Application Timing:** Rolling/ongoing

- **Pre-Application:** Contact EFI anytime
- **Formal Application:** Submit when project details finalized
- **Review Period:** 60-120 days typical
- **Negotiation:** Varies by project complexity
- **Agreement Execution:** Upon successful negotiation

**Note:** Projects with tight timelines may qualify for expedited review

## Tips for Success

1. **Engage Early** - Contact EFI before finalizing project details
2. **Think Big** - Incentives scale with project size
