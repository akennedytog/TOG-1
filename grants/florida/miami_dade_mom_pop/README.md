# Miami-Dade Mom & Pop Small Business Grant

## Program Overview
**Program Type:** Small business grant for local Miami-Dade businesses  
**Target:** Small "mom and pop" businesses with physical storefronts  
**Use:** Business improvements, equipment, working capital

## Eligibility Checklist

### Business Requirements
- [ ] Physically located in Miami-Dade County
- [ ] For-profit business with brick-and-mortar location
- [ ] Business license current and in good standing
- [ ] Annual gross revenue under $[check current limit - typically $500K-$1M]
- [ ] In operation for minimum period (typically 1-2 years)
- [ ] Current on all taxes and fees
- [ ] Not a franchise or chain location
- [ ] Owner-operated business

### Location Requirements
- [ ] Commercial property located in Miami-Dade County
- [ ] Business signage visible from street
- [ ] Open to the public
- [ ] Not home-based (typically excluded)

### AI Automation Company Eligibility Assessment
- [ ] **CONDITIONALLY ELIGIBLE** - Physical office required
- [ ] Must have commercial lease in Miami-Dade
- [ ] May need to demonstrate direct public/customer access
- [ ] Revenue cap may exclude fast-growing tech companies

**Note:** Tech companies often struggle with "mom and pop" designation. Consider:
- Applying as a service provider to local businesses
- Emphasizing job creation for other local small businesses
- Partnering with a qualifying business as co-applicant

## Required Documents

1. **Completed Application Form** (from Miami-Dade Economic Development)
2. **Business Documentation**
   - Current Miami-Dade Business Tax Receipt (BTR)
   - State of Florida Business Registration
   - Fictitious Name Registration (if applicable)
   - Articles of Incorporation/Organization
3. **Financial Documents**
   - Last 2 years of business tax returns
   - Current financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet)
   - Last 6 months of business bank statements
4. **Lease/Property Documents**
   - Commercial lease agreement or property deed
   - Property tax receipt (if owner-occupied)
   - Photos of business exterior and interior
5. **Personal Documentation**
   - Owner's driver's license or ID
   - Proof of Miami-Dade residency
6. **Grant Use Plan**
   - Detailed budget for grant funds
   - Quotes for equipment/services
   - Timeline for project completion
7. **Letters of Support**
   - From local business association or chamber
   - From customers or community members

## Step-by-Step Application Guide

### Step 1: Verify Eligibility (2-3 weeks before deadline)
1. Review complete eligibility requirements on miamidade.gov
2. Confirm business license is current
3. Verify tax compliance with Miami-Dade Tax Collector
4. Confirm revenue falls within program limits

### Step 2: Gather Documentation (2-3 weeks)
1. Request tax transcripts from IRS (if needed)
2. Update financial statements
3. Obtain current quotes for planned purchases
4. Draft letters of support
5. Take current photos of business location

### Step 3: Complete Application (1-2 weeks)
1. Download current application form from Miami-Dade website
2. Complete all sections thoroughly
3. Attach all required documentation
4. Have business partner/attorney review
5. Obtain required signatures

### Step 4: Submit Application
1. Submit via online portal or in-person at:
   - Miami-Dade Economic Advocacy Trust (if applicable)
   - Or designated county office
2. Keep copies of all submitted materials
3. Request confirmation of receipt

### Step 5: Follow Up
1. Note application tracking number
2. Respond promptly to any requests for additional information
3. Prepare for potential site visit
