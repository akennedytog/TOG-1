# Florida SBDC Network Programs

## Program Overview
**Organization:** Florida Small Business Development Center Network  
**Services:** No-cost consulting, training, and specialized programs  
**Coverage:** 9 regional centers serving all 67 Florida counties  
**Cost:** FREE to Florida businesses

## Available Programs

### 1. Specialized Business Consulting
- Growth Acceleration
- Business Planning
- Financial Management
- Marketing Strategy
- Export Assistance
- Government Contracting
- Cybersecurity Assessment

### 2. Capital Access Programs
- **Bridge Loans** - Short-term financing assistance
- **Loan Packaging** - Help prepare loan applications
- **Alternative Finance** - Crowdfunding, factoring, etc.

### 3. Specialized Initiatives
- **Digital Marketing Program** - Online strategy development
- **Veterans Business Outreach** - Specialized veteran services
- **International Trade** - Export counseling
- **Minority Business Development** - MBE/DBE certification help

### 4. Training & Workshops
- Business planning workshops
- QuickBooks training
- Marketing seminars
- Financial management courses
- Startup training

## Eligibility Checklist

### General SBDC Services
- [x] **OPEN TO ALL** Florida businesses

## Required Documents

### For Consulting Services
1. **Business Registration**
   - Articles of Incorporation/Organization
   - Business licenses
2. **Current Business Plan** (or draft)
3. **Financial Statements**
   - Last 2 years tax returns
   - Current P&L and Balance Sheet
4. **Project Description** (for specialized consulting)

### For Loan Packaging
1. **Personal Financial Statement** (owner)
2. **Business Financial Statements** (3 years if available)
3. **Tax Returns** (business and personal, last 3 years)
4. **Business Plan with Financial Projections**
5. **Use of Funds Statement**
6. **Collateral Documentation**
7. **Accounts Receivable/Payable Aging**
8. **Bank Statements** (last 6 months)

### For Growth Acceleration
1. **Strategic Growth Plan**
2. **Market Research**
3. **Competitive Analysis**
4. **Management Team Bios**
5. **Detailed Financial History**
6. **Growth Projections**
