# The One Group - 3-Minute Pitch Script

---

## Opening (15 seconds)

"Good morning. I'm [Name], and I'm here to introduce you to the future of marketing.

Every day, businesses burn thousands of dollars on marketing agencies that take weeks to execute, work 8-hour days, and charge $50,000 a month for inconsistent results."

---

## Problem (30 seconds)

"Here's the problem: Traditional marketing agencies are fundamentally limited by humans. They can only work when people are awake, they get burned out, and every new client means expensive new hires.

The result? 40% of agency revenue goes to labor costs. Campaigns take weeks. Results are inconsistent. And scaling? That's just a fancy word for 'hire more people.'

This isn't sustainable. And in 2024, it shouldn't be necessary."

---

## Solution (45 seconds)

"We built The One Group — the world's first fully autonomous AI agency.

Instead of people, we have eight specialized AI agents working together 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

We have a Lead Generation agent that finds prospects. A Research agent that learns everything about them. A Creative agent that writes personalized outreach. An Outreach agent that sends emails. A Follow-up agent that nurtures leads. Analytics, Optimization, Content — all agents, all autonomous, all working together.

These agents don't sleep. They don't take vacations. They don't burn out. And they communicate with each other in real-time, learning from every interaction to get better every day."

---

## Traction (30 seconds)

"And it's working.

We're generating 10 qualified leads every single day. Our email response rate is 23% — that's nearly 8 times the industry average. We've already built a $50,000 pipeline in our first month.

Most importantly? Zero human intervention required."

---

## Market & Business Model (30 seconds)

"This is a $350 billion market, and we're targeting the $50 billion slice spent by SMBs on marketing services.

We charge $2,000 to $10,000 a month depending on the package — that's 60 to 90 percent less than traditional agencies. Our gross margins are 85% because we're not paying for human labor.

Customer acquisition cost is $2,000. Lifetime value is $24,000. That's a 12-to-1 ratio."

---

## Competitive Advantage (20 seconds)

"There are AI marketing tools, but they're point solutions. One tool for email, another for analytics, another for content. None of them work together.

We're the only company with a coordinated agent swarm architecture. That's our moat."

---

## The Ask (10 seconds)

"We're raising $500,000 to scale from 10 to 100 customers in the next 12 months and hit $1 million ARR.

Thank you."

---

## Closing (10 seconds)

"The One Group — eight agents, infinite possibilities, zero excuses.

Questions?"

---

## Timing Notes

- Opening: 0:00-0:15
- Problem: 0:15-0:45
- Solution: 0:45-1:30
- Traction: 1:30-2:00
- Market/Model: 2:00-2:30
- Advantage: 2:30-2:50
- Ask: 2:50-3:00
- Total: Exactly 3 minutes

## Delivery Tips

1. **Pace:** Speak deliberately. 3 minutes is plenty of time.
2. **Pause:** After "10 qualified leads every single day" — let it land.
3. **Energy:** Build from problem → solution → traction crescendo.
4. **Eye contact:** Look at judges, not slides.
5. **End strong:** The "eight agents, infinite possibilities, zero excuses" line should be memorable.
