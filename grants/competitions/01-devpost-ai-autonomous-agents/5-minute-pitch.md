# The One Group - 5-Minute Pitch Script

---

## Opening (20 seconds)

"Good morning. I'm [Name], and I'm here because I believe marketing agencies as we know them are about to disappear.

Not because they want to. But because they have to.

Let me show you why."

---

## Problem (60 seconds)

"Traditional marketing agencies are hitting a wall. And that wall is made of humans.

Think about how a typical agency works. You hire them for $5,000, $20,000, sometimes $50,000 a month. They assign you an account manager — one person who needs to sleep, eat, take weekends off, and occasionally gets sick.

That person needs to coordinate with a copywriter, a designer, a strategist, an analyst. Each working 9 to 5. Each juggling 10 other clients. Each getting burned out.

When you want to scale? Hire more people. More salaries. More benefits. More management overhead. More chances for things to fall through the cracks.

The math doesn't work. Forty percent of agency revenue goes to labor costs alone. Campaigns that should take days take weeks. And the results? They depend entirely on who's having a good day.

This is a $350 billion industry running on a broken model."

---

## Solution (90 seconds)

"So we asked: What if we replaced the entire agency with AI?

Not one tool. Not one chatbot. A complete agency.

Meet The One Group — the world's first fully autonomous AI agency.

Instead of humans, we built eight specialized AI agents. Think of them as employees who never sleep, never complain, and get smarter every single day.

The Lead Generation agent scours the internet finding your ideal customers. The Research agent learns everything about them — their company, their pain points, their recent news.

The Creative agent writes personalized outreach that doesn't feel like spam. The Outreach agent sends it at the perfect time. The Follow-up agent nurtures leads with perfectly timed sequences.

Meanwhile, the Analytics agent tracks every open, every click, every reply. The Optimization agent uses that data to improve everything in real-time. The Content agent creates blog posts, social content, ad copy.

And they all talk to each other. Constantly.

When the Research agent learns a prospect just raised funding, it tells the Creative agent to mention it in the email. When the Analytics agent sees subject line A performing better, it tells Outreach to use it more. When Follow-up notices a lead went cold, it alerts Content to create a re-engagement piece.

This isn't a pipeline. It's a swarm. Intelligent, adaptive, autonomous."

---

## Traction & Validation (45 seconds)

"We've been running this system for three months. Here are the numbers:

Ten qualified leads. Every. Single. Day. Generated while we sleep.

Our email response rate is twenty-three percent. The industry average is three percent. We're doing eight times better.

We've built over $50,000 in pipeline value in month one alone.

And here's the kicker — this entire operation runs with zero human intervention. I literally check the dashboard in the morning to see what the agents accomplished overnight.

This isn't theoretical. This is working. Right now."

---

## Market & Business Model (45 seconds)

"The marketing services market is $350 billion globally. But we're not trying to serve everyone.

We're focused on SMBs — companies spending $3,000 to $20,000 a month on marketing who aren't getting results. That's a $50 billion serviceable market.

Our pricing is simple. Starter package: $2,000 a month. Growth: $5,000. Enterprise: $10,000. That's 60 to 90 percent less than traditional agencies charge.

But here's why the unit economics are beautiful: Our gross margin is 85%. Because we're not paying for people.

Customer acquisition cost is $2,000. Lifetime value is $24,000. That's a 12-to-1 ratio. When we spend $1 on marketing, we get $12 back over the customer's lifetime."

---

## Competitive Advantage (45 seconds)

"Now, you might be thinking — aren't there already AI marketing tools?

Yes. Hundreds of them. But they're all point solutions. One tool for email. Another for analytics. Another for social media. They don't talk to each other. They don't coordinate. They don't learn from each other.

What we've built is fundamentally different. It's not eight separate tools. It's one coordinated swarm.

The technical moat is our agent coordination architecture — the system that lets these agents communicate, delegate, and optimize as a collective. We've been training this system for six months. That's six months of data and learning that would take a competitor years to replicate.

Plus, every day our agents run, they get smarter. The gap widens."

---

## The Team (15 seconds)

"We're builders. Our technical lead spent 10 years at Amazon building distributed systems. Our product lead scaled a SaaS company from zero to $10 million ARR. Our GTM lead closed over $100 million in enterprise contracts.

We're not theorists. We've done this before."

---

## The Ask (20 seconds)

"Today, we're raising $500,000 in pre-seed funding.

Forty percent goes to engineering — improving the agents, adding new verticals, making them smarter. Thirty percent to customer acquisition — paid ads, partnerships, scaling our own lead gen. Twenty percent to infrastructure — reliability at scale. Ten percent to operations.

Our milestones are clear: 100 paying customers in 12 months. $1 million ARR by month 18. Series A ready."

---

## Closing (20 seconds)

"The future of marketing isn't more people sitting in more meetings. It's intelligent systems that work while you sleep.

The One Group — eight agents, infinite possibilities, zero excuses.

We're not just building a better agency. We're building the last agency you'll ever need.

Thank you. I'd love to take your questions."

## Key Phrases to Emphasize

1. "Zero human intervention required" — pause after this
2. "Ten qualified leads. Every. Single. Day." — use rhythm
3. "This isn't a pipeline. It's a swarm." — the core concept
4. "Eight agents, infinite possibilities, zero excuses" — tagline

## Visual Cues

- When describing the 8 agents, gesture with both hands spread
- For traction numbers, point to imaginary stats on screen
- When saying "zero human intervention," shake head slowly for emphasis
