## Judging Preparation

### Demo Day Checklist
- [ ] Arrive 30 minutes early
- [ ] Test AV equipment
- [ ] Have backup slides ready
- [ ] Print FAQ reference cards
- [ ] Bring business cards
- [ ] Prepare 60-second elevator pitch
- [ ] Practice Q&A responses
- [ ] Charge all devices
- [ ] Bring water
- [ ] Smile and have fun!

### What Judges Look For
- Innovation: Is this new/different?
- Technical difficulty: How hard was this to build?
- Impact: Does it solve a real problem?
- Polish: Is it presented professionally?
- Viability: Could this become a business?

### Questions to Anticipate
1. "How is this different from [competitor]?"
2. "What's your technical moat?"
3. "How do you prevent spam?"
4. "What's stopping someone from copying this?"
5. "Who are your customers?"
6. "What happens if an agent makes a mistake?"
