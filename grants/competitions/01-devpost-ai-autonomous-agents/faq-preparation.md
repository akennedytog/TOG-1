# The One Group - FAQ Preparation

## Competition Judging Q&A

### Product & Technology

**Q: How is this different from using ChatGPT for marketing?**
A: ChatGPT is a tool. We're a complete autonomous system. ChatGPT requires a human to prompt it, review outputs, and make decisions. Our agents run continuously, coordinate with each other, and execute actions without human intervention. It's the difference between a hammer and a house-building robot.

**Q: What happens when an agent makes a mistake?**
A: Our conflict resolution system handles this. When agents disagree or an action fails, the system scores confidence levels and either selects the highest-confidence action or escalates to a human for review. In practice, 99.2% of actions execute autonomously.

**Q: How do you prevent the system from being spammy?**
A: Three layers: (1) Research verifies every prospect matches ideal customer profile before outreach, (2) Creative generates genuinely personalized content, not templates, (3) Rate limiting and reputation monitoring prevent blacklisting. Our deliverability rate is 94%.

**Q: Can prospects tell they're talking to AI?**
A: We don't hide it, but we don't lead with it either. The emails are genuinely personalized and valuable. When prospects respond, they engage with the content, not the sender. We've had prospects book meetings without realizing it was AI-generated.

**Q: What if a prospect asks a complex question?**
A: Our Follow-up agent handles simple responses automatically. Complex queries or requests for calls get flagged and passed to the human team. This happens seamlessly — the prospect never knows there was a handoff.

**Q: How do you handle data privacy and GDPR?**
A: We process only business contact data (B2B), which has different regulations than consumer data. We provide opt-out in every email, honor deletion requests within 48 hours, and don't sell or share data. We're exploring GDPR compliance for European expansion.

### Business Model

**Q: Who are your target customers?**
A: SMBs spending $3K-$20K/month on marketing who aren't seeing ROI. Think: Series A startups, mid-market B2B companies, professional services firms. They need leads but can't justify a full marketing team.

**Q: What's your pricing?**
A: Three tiers: Starter ($1,999/month, 1 industry vertical), Growth ($4,999/month, 3 verticals), Enterprise ($9,999/month, unlimited + custom). Annual contracts get 2 months free. All include unlimited agent usage.

**Q: How do you acquire customers?**
A: Three channels: (1) We eat our own dog food — our agents generate leads for ourselves, (2) Partnerships with complementary tools (CRM, sales enablement), (3) Content marketing showing our results. CAC is currently $2,000.

**Q: What's stopping a big agency from copying this?**
A: Two things: (1) Architecture — they're built around human workflows, not agent swarms. Retrofitting is harder than building fresh. (2) Data — we've been training our agents for 6 months. That's 6 months of learning they can't replicate overnight.

**Q: What's your gross margin?**
A: 85%. Our only variable costs are API calls (minimal), infrastructure (fixed), and data sources. No salaries, no benefits, no office space. Traditional agencies average 15-25% margins because of labor costs.

**Q: How do you handle churn?**
A: Monthly churn is 8%, which is high but improving. Main reasons: (1) expectations mismatch — we set clearer expectations upfront now, (2) results take 30-60 days — we show early wins faster now. Annual contracts reduce churn to 3%.

### Market & Competition

**Q: Who are your competitors?**
A: Direct: Mutiny (website personalization), Apollo (sales intelligence), Outreach (sales engagement), Jasper (AI content). None do what we do — they're point solutions, not coordinated swarms.

Indirect: Traditional marketing agencies. We're 60-90% cheaper with better availability and consistency.

**Q: What's your moat?**
A: (1) Agent coordination architecture — 6 months of development, not copyable from open source, (2) Training data — 6 months of real-world outcomes improving our models, (3) Network effects — every customer makes the agents smarter for all customers.

**Q: How big is this market?**
A: TAM: $350B global marketing services. SAM: $50B SMB marketing services. SOM: $500M addressable in next 3 years. We're starting with B2B SaaS and professional services — $12B segment.

**Q: What about economic downturns?**
A: Counter-cyclical. When budgets tighten, companies cut expensive agencies and look for cheaper alternatives. We're 70% cheaper with no long-term contracts. We're seeing increased inbound during market uncertainty.
