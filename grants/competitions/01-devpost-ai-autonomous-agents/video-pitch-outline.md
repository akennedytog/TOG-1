## Production Notes

### Equipment
- **Camera:** 1080p webcam or DSLR
- **Microphone:** USB condenser mic (clear audio critical)
- **Lighting:** Natural light or ring light (face well-lit)
- **Background:** Clean, professional (not distracting)

### Technical Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps minimum
- **Audio:** 48kHz, noise-reduced
- **Export:** MP4, H.264 codec

### Screen Recording
- **Software:** OBS, Loom, or ScreenFlow
- **Cursor:** Highlight clicks with visual indicator
- **Zoom:** Smooth zoom on important UI elements
- **Transitions:** Simple cuts, no fancy effects needed

### Editing Tips
1. **Pacing:** Keep energy high, no dead air
2. **B-Roll:** Cut to dashboard/screenshots during narration
3. **Text Overlays:** Emphasize key numbers
4. **Music:** Upbeat, instrumental, not distracting
5. **Captions:** Always include for accessibility

### Review Checklist
- [ ] Audio is clear and balanced
- [ ] Screen text is readable
- [ ] Cursor movements are smooth
- [ ] No personal/confidential data visible
- [ ] Logo/end card included
- [ ] Total runtime 3-4 minutes
- [ ] Export quality appropriate for platform

## Platform-Specific Notes

**Devpost:**
- Max file size: 100MB
- Recommended: Upload to YouTube/Vimeo, embed link

**General Competitions:**
- Have both 3-minute and extended versions
- Prepare thumbnail image
- Write compelling video title/description

**Live Pitch Backup:**
- Have video on local device (not just cloud)
- Test playback before presenting
- Have slides as backup if video fails
