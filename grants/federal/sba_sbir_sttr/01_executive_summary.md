## THE PROBLEM

Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) constitute 99.9% of all U.S. businesses and employ 61.6 million Americans, yet they face a critical operational paradox. While large enterprises invest millions in AI and automation to streamline operations, SMBs remain trapped in manual workflows because existing solutions are either too expensive, too complex, or too limited in capability.

The average SMB spends 120 hours per month on administrative tasks—time that could be directed toward growth and customer acquisition. This "automation gap" limits SMB competitiveness and constrains economic growth in communities nationwide.

## THE SOLUTION

The One Group proposes an Autonomous AI Agent Platform specifically engineered for SMB operational needs. Unlike simple automation tools that handle isolated tasks, our platform deploys intelligent agent swarms that understand complete business contexts, make autonomous decisions within defined parameters, and continuously improve through reinforcement learning.

Our technical innovation rests on three pillars:
1. **Multi-Agent Orchestration**: Coordinated AI agents that collaborate across business functions
2. **Zero-Code Configuration**: Natural language interfaces enabling non-technical users to define agent behaviors
3. **SMB-Optimized Learning**: Transfer learning from foundation models adapted to SMB-specific constraints
