## C. BUDGET NARRATIVE

### Cost Reasonableness

The budget of $295,000 for a 6-month Phase I project is consistent with NSF SBIR guidelines and typical awards in the $256K-$295K range. The budget reflects:

1. **Lean team structure**: 4 FTE-equivalent personnel focused on core technical objectives
2. **Efficient infrastructure**: Leveraging cloud credits and existing equipment where possible
3. **Targeted scope**: Focus on validating core hypotheses rather than full product development

### Personnel Effort Allocation

Total effort is calibrated to accomplish Phase I objectives:
- PI oversight ensures strategic alignment (50% effort sufficient)
- Technical leads dedicate substantial time to innovation (50-100% effort)
- Research validation without over-engineering (part-time UX)

### Cost Share

The One Group will contribute cost share through:
- Existing cloud credits: $35,000 value
- Office space and utilities: $7,200 (6 months)
- Existing equipment use: $5,000 value
- **Total Cost Share: $47,200**

This demonstrates commitment beyond the requested funding.

### Budget Flexibility

We understand that NSF may negotiate this budget. Priority for reductions (in order):
1. Equipment (can lease or use cloud alternatives): -$8,000
2. Travel (increase virtual meetings): -$3,000
3. Indirect rate (accept lower): -$8,000

Core technical personnel and direct R&D costs are essential and not negotiable.
