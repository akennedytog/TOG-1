### 1.3 Company History and Mission

**Founded:** 2024  
**Mission Statement:** Democratizing access to enterprise-grade AI automation for small and medium-sized businesses, enabling them to compete effectively in an increasingly digital economy.

**Company Narrative:**

The One Group was founded by Alec Kennedy with a clear observation: while large enterprises invest millions in AI and automation to streamline operations, small businesses—the backbone of the American economy—remain trapped in manual workflows, unable to afford sophisticated solutions or the technical talent to implement them.

After exiting previous ventures and extensive research into the SMB automation gap, Alec assembled a team of AI researchers, software engineers, and business operators to build what would become The One Group's flagship platform: an autonomous AI agent system specifically architected for SMB operational needs.

The company is currently in pre-launch development, with Phase I of our product underway and pilot customer relationships being established.
