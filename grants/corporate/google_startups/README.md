## Step-by-Step Application Walkthrough

### Understanding the Program

Google for Startups Cloud has multiple tiers:
- **$1,000** - Self-serve (any startup)
- **$10,000** - Series A and beyond
- **$100,000-$200,000** - Accelerator/portfolio companies

**Target: $200,000** - Requires joining Google partner accelerator or portfolio

### Path to $200K Credits:

**Option 1: Google for Startups Accelerator** (Recommended)
- Apply to Google for Startups Accelerator
- 10-week virtual program
- $200K in credits upon acceptance
- Mentorship, technical support, Google introductions
- URL: https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerators/

**Option 2: Portfolio Company**
- Join VC that partners with Google
- Many top-tier VCs have Google partnerships
- Check if your investors participate

**Option 3: Google Cloud Partners**
- Some Google Cloud consulting partners can grant credits
- Typically smaller amounts ($10K-$50K)

### Application Process (Accelerator Route):

1. **Prepare Application Materials**
   - Company profile (see above)
   - Demo video (2-3 minutes)
   - Founder bios
   - Current metrics (even if early)
   - Why Google Cloud specifically

2. **Complete Online Application**
   - URL: https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerators/
   - Select appropriate region/program
   - Upload all materials
   - Submit before deadline

3. **Interview Process**
   - If selected, 30-45 minute interview
   - Technical and business questions
   - Demo of product

4. **Program Participation**
   - 10-week commitment
   - Weekly sessions
   - Assigned Google mentors
   - Credits distributed during program

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Critical Mistakes

1. **Generic "Why Google" Answer**
   - Don't say "credits are bigger than AWS"
   - Reference specific services: Vertex AI, Gemini, Workspace integration
   - Show you've researched their ecosystem

2. **No Clear Product**
   - Must have MVP or working product
   - Ideas alone won't get accepted
   - Demo should work end-to-end

3. **Wrong Stage for Accelerator**
   - Too early: Just an idea
   - Too late: Already raised Series B
   - Sweet spot: Seed/Pre-Series A with product and early traction

4. **Ignoring the Commitment**
   - Accelerator requires 10 weeks of participation
   - Missing sessions reflects poorly
   - Only apply if you can commit

5. **Poor Technical Fit**
   - Don't apply if you're not planning to use Google Cloud
   - They want companies building ON their platform

### ⚠️ Important Notes

- Accelerator cohorts run 2-3x per year
- Credits valid for 1-2 years typically
- Must be incorporated (not sole proprietorship)
- Google Workspace integration is a plus for your use case

### ✅ Pro Tips

1. **Emphasize Workspace Integration**
   - Millions of SMBs use Google Workspace
   - Your product helps them get more from it
   - This aligns with Google's strategy

2. **Mention Vertex AI Specifically**
   - Google's flagship AI platform
   - Shows deep understanding of their stack

3. **Highlight Google Partnership Potential**
   - Could you become a Workspace partner?
   - Mention ecosystem play

4. **Build Before Applying**
   - Have working prototype using Google Cloud
   - Even $100 in personal credits shows commitment

5. **Network First**
   - Attend Google for Startups events
   - Connect with alumni founders
   - Get warm introductions if possible
