## Critical Positioning Notes

### The "Agency" Problem

Several programs exclude "consulting firms" or "agencies." Reposition The One Group:

**Instead of:** "AI automation agency for SMBs"  
**Use:** "AI automation platform for SMBs" or "AI-powered workflow automation software"

**Key distinctions to emphasize:**
- Product/platform over services
- Self-serve capabilities
- Scalable software, not custom consulting
- Recurring SaaS revenue model

## Common Rejection Reasons

### Technical
- ❌ No working product (just an idea)
- ❌ Website not live
- ❌ Generic use case descriptions

### Business
- ❌ Positioned as services/consulting
- ❌ No clear market opportunity
- ❌ Team lacks technical depth

### Process
- ❌ Missing referral (AWS)
- ❌ Wrong stage (too early or too late)
- ❌ Incomplete application
