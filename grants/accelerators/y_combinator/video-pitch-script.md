# Y Combinator Video Pitch Script
**The One Group** - 60 Seconds  
*To be recorded on Loom/YouTube*

---

## Opening Hook (0-10 seconds)

"Small businesses waste 10+ hours a week on repetitive tasks—scheduling, follow-ups, reporting. They can't afford $50k enterprise automation, and cheap AI tools are too complicated. They're stuck."

---

## Problem Validation (10-25 seconds)

"I watched my parents run a small HVAC business for 20 years. They knew automation would help, but everything they tried was either too expensive or required a CS degree to set up. So they just... kept doing it manually."

---

## Solution + Demo (25-45 seconds)

"The One Group builds AI agents that handle repetitive tasks and actually get used. Here's our agent scheduling appointments—it reads emails, checks calendars, suggests times, and confirms bookings. All through email, no new interface to learn.

Our secret sauce is Paperclip—our orchestration system that runs multi-agent workflows at near-zero cost. We use free local AI for 95% of tasks, so we're profitable even at low prices."

---

## Traction + Ask (45-60 seconds)

"In one month, we've closed $5k in revenue, generated 15 qualified leads, and have a waiting list of South Florida businesses. We're the AI-Native Agency YC is looking for, and we're ready to scale.

I'm Alec Kennedy, and we're The One Group."

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Lighting:** Face a window or use ring light
2. **Audio:** Use headphones with mic or external mic
3. **Background:** Clean, professional (or blurred)
4. **Energy:** Enthusiastic but not fake
5. **Pacing:** Practice until it flows naturally

## Alternative Opening Lines

- "Most AI tools fail because SMBs abandon them. We've solved that."
- "What if AI automation just... worked?"
- "10 million small businesses are drowning in repetitive work. We're building the lifeline."

## Alternative Closings

- "We're not just building AI. We're building AI that SMBs actually use."
- "AI-Native Agency. Real results. Let's build something big."

---

*Script duration: ~55 seconds at normal pace*  
*Practice 5+ times before recording*
