## Demo Script (3 minutes)

### Setup
"Let me show you Paperclip in action. This is our orchestration system managing an AI agent."

### The Demo
1. **Show incoming email:** "A client wants to schedule a consultation."
2. **Show agent processing:** "Paperclip routes this to the scheduling agent."
3. **Show agent actions:** 
   - Reads email (natural language understanding)
   - Checks calendar (API integration)
   - Drafts response (personalized, professional)
   - Proposes 3 time slots
4. **Show cost:** "This entire interaction cost $0.02. On GPT-4, it would be $0.50+."
5. **Show scale:** "One agent handles 100 of these per day. Paperclip manages 100 agents."

### Closing
"That's not a chatbot. That's an autonomous worker that integrates into existing workflows. And we're just getting started."

## Backup Plan

**If live demo fails:**
- Have screen recording ready
- Say: "Let me show you a recorded version—this is what our agents do live."
- Keep energy high, don't apologize excessively

**If technical question stumps:**
- "Great question—let me follow up on that."
- Don't bluff
- Show depth where you have it
