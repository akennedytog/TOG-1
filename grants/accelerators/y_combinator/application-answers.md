## Idea

### Why did you pick this idea to work on?

Three converging observations:

1. **SMBs are drowning in repetitive work** but can't afford enterprise automation ($50k+ implementations). They need affordable AI that actually works.

2. **Current AI tools fail because** they're generic and don't integrate into workflows. SMBs abandon them because setup is complex and results are disappointing.

3. **South Florida is underserved**—a massive market of lifestyle businesses (HVAC, legal, real estate, professional services) with money to spend and repetitive tasks to automate.

We've personally seen friends and family running SMBs waste hours on scheduling, follow-ups, and reporting when AI could handle it. But existing solutions either require technical expertise or cost too much.

### What do you understand about this business that other companies just don't get?

**Adoption is the real moat.** Most AI companies focus on building the best model. We focus on making sure SMBs actually *use* the AI.

Our insight: SMBs don't want to learn new tools. They want their existing problems solved. So we:
- Integrate into workflows they already have (no new logins, no new interfaces)
- Start with one specific use case (e.g., scheduling), prove value, then expand
- Use human-in-the-loop to build trust before full automation
- Optimize for cost (Ollama) so we're profitable even at low price points

This makes us sticky. Once we're handling their scheduling, we become the obvious choice for follow-ups, then reporting, then competitor monitoring.

### Who are your competitors?

**Direct:**
- Zapier + AI (generic, requires setup)
- Jasper/Copy.ai (content only, no workflow integration)
- Lindy/MindStudio (still require technical users)

**Adjacent:**
- Traditional automation agencies (expensive, slow)
- Big consulting firms (overkill for SMBs)
- Freelancers (inconsistent, don't scale)

**Differentiation:**
We combine AI capabilities with human judgment and workflow integration. We're not just AI—we're *AI that SMBs actually use.*

### What do you fear?

1. **Big players entering the SMB space** (Salesforce, HubSpot) but they're slow and focused on enterprise
2. **AI commoditization** but our moat is workflow integration, not just AI
3. **Customer acquisition costs rising** but our referral program and content strategy keep CAC low
4. **Technical talent** but our cost discipline lets us hire slower
