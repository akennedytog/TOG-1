# "Back the Founder" — Founder Scorecard Design (v1)

**Purpose:** The scorecard is the *product*. It's what makes a founder credible + compelling on the feed, and it's the defensible moat (nobody has cracked objective founder-quality scoring). Design principle: **verifiable > vibe.** If it's vibes, it's a pump racket. If it's rigorous, it's a genuine signal.

**Format note:** Two layers visible to users:
1. **Public scorecard** — what backers see & judge (compelling, easy to scan)
2. **Internal scoring model** — what we score behind the scenes to vet/rank (rigorous, defensible)

---

## PART A — Public Scorecard (what backers see)

### 1. Identity & trust (the "is this real?" row)
- **Name + photo** (verified)
- **One-line role** — e.g. "Serial operator → building X"
- **Verified links** — LinkedIn, website, portfolio
- **Consent badge** — "opted in to be public" (protects us + them)

### 2. The Project (the "what am I backing?" row)
- **Project name** + one-line pitch (compelling, specific — NOT "I want to be rich")
- **Stage** — Idea / Built / Revenue / Scaling (a clear, single-select tag)
- **Current focus** — what they're building *right now* (the timeline hook)
- **Ask** — what would backing actually support? (tooling, runway, a milestone, a launch)

### 3. Traction (the "is it working?" row)
- **Revenue** (if any) — monthly, honest
- **Users / customers** — count + trajectory
- **Milestones hit** — dated, with proof links
- **Milestone velocity** — how fast are they actually shipping (this predicts more than the raw number)

### 4. Track record (the "have they done this?" row)
- **Prior work** — past companies, projects, roles
- **Proof of execution** — shipped products, revenue history, references
- **Why them** — founder's own case for why they're worth backing (compelling, specific)

### 5. Momentum & community (the "are others in?" row)
- **Backer count** + top backers (leaderboard)
- **Backed since / current boost total**
- **Update streak** — how consistently they post (consistency = signal)
- **Milestone hits** — "2 of 5 milestones achieved"

**How it looks:** a clean card, scannable in ~10 seconds, one thing that pops. Not a wall of data — a *verdict* with evidence behind it.

---

## B — Internal Scoring Model (what we compute)

This is the defensible core. A **weighted score** that ranks founders without gaming. It's never shown raw to users as "this founder is worth X" (legal/fraud risk + it invites gaming) — it feeds curation & ordering.

### The 5 weighted pillars (draft weights)
| Pillar | Weight | What we score |
|---|---|---|
| **Track record** | 25% | Past execution, proof of shipping, references |
| **Momentum** | 25% | Revenue/user trajectory + milestone velocity (not raw numbers) |
| **Vetting completeness** | 20% | How fully verified the profile is (identity, docs, refs) |
| **Engagement quality** | 20% | Update consistency, backer response, community signal |
| **Coachability / credibility** | 10% | Founder's case quality, specific asks, realistic milestones |

### Anti-gaming rules (critical)
- **Never publish a single "founder score"** as a rating users bet on — publishes = invites manipulation + fraud liability. Scores stay internal; users see *evidence*, not verdicts.
- **Momentum ≠ vanity metrics** — weight velocity and proof, not just followers/customers (fakeable).
- **Vetting is recurring** — scores refresh; a founder who stops updating decays.
- **Human override** — a credible operator (Alec) can flag/verify edge cases.

---

## C — The "Verdict Card" (what a user actually reads)

Keep the *product* simple. A backer should be able to glance and think: *"This founder is real, is building, and other people are in."*

**A founder card shows:**
- Photo + name + role
- One-line project pitch
- Stage tag (Idea / Building / Revenue / Scaling)
- "Backed by N · $X" + top backers
- Milestone progress bar (3 of 5)
- One "latest update" teaser

**That's it.** No dense scorecard dump. The full scorecard is behind a tap.
