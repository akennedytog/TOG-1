# South Florida Lead Generation Strategy

## Target Market
**Location:** Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach Counties
**Business Size:** 10-100 employees
**Industries:** HVAC, Legal, Accounting, Real Estate, E-commerce, Professional Services

## Target Personas
1. **Business Owner** - Decision maker, 40-60 years old, tech-curious but time-starved
2. **Operations Manager** - Handles efficiency, looking for cost savings
3. **Marketing Director** - Wants competitive edge in AI visibility

## Value Props for South Florida Market
1. **"Beat the Heat"** - HVAC automation for busy season
2. **"Stand Out in Miami"** - AI visibility in competitive market
3. **"Work Smarter, Not Harder"** - Automation for lifestyle businesses
4. **"Stay Competitive"** - Competitors are already using AI

## Lead Sources
1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** - Target by location + title
2. **Google Maps** - Local business scraping
3. **Chamber of Commerce** - Member directories
4. **Industry Associations** - FLBAR, HVAC associations
5. **Referrals** - Existing client network
6. **Events** - Miami Tech Meetups, Business expos

## Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1: Warm Introduction (Week 1)
- Personalized LinkedIn connection
- Mention South Florida connection
- No pitch, just connection

### Phase 2: Value First (Week 2)
- Share relevant article/insight
- Comment on their posts
- Build relationship

### Phase 3: Soft Pitch (Week 3)
- "I help South Florida businesses automate..."
- Offer free AI visibility audit
- Calendly link

### Phase 4: Follow Up (Week 4+)
- Weekly touch points
- Case studies
- Industry-specific insights

## Automation Workflow
1. **Daily:** Scrape 20 new leads
2. **Daily:** Send 5 LinkedIn connections
3. **Daily:** Follow up on existing leads
4. **Weekly:** Send personalized emails
5. **Weekly:** Post South Florida-specific content

## Metrics to Track
- Connection acceptance rate
- Response rate
- Meeting booked rate
- Close rate by source
- Cost per lead
- Time to close

## South Florida Specific Angles
- **Hurricane prep** - "Is your business ready for busy season?"
- **Tourism season** - "Handle peak demand with AI"
- **Competition** - "Miami businesses are adopting AI"
- **Lifestyle** - "Work less, earn more"
