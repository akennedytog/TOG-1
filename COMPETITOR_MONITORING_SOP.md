## CLIENT ONBOARDING (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Discovery Call
**Questions to ask:**
1. "Who are your top 3 direct competitors?"
2. "Any emerging competitors you're watching?"
3. "What AI queries would you expect to show up for?" (e.g., "best HVAC in Louisville")
4. "What's your biggest concern about competitors?"

### Step 2: Competitor Selection
**Track 3-5 competitors:**
- 2-3 direct competitors (same service, same area)
- 1-2 emerging threats (newer, growing fast)
- Optional: 1 "aspirational" competitor (bigger, where you want to be)

### Step 3: Baseline Audit
**Run full AI Visibility Audit on:**
- Client's website (baseline)
- All 3-5 competitors

**Record in tracker:**
- Initial scores
- Key findings
- Priority areas

## REAL-TIME ALERTS

### When to Send Immediate Alerts

**Send alert when:**
- Competitor score changes by >10 points
- Competitor gains major new citation (e.g., featured in major publication)
- Competitor launches new AI-optimized content strategy
- Competitor appears in AI responses for new keywords
- Your client drops in ranking

## MONTHLY WORKFLOW

### Week 1: Data Collection (1 hour)
**Day 1-3:**
- [ ] Re-run AI Visibility Audit on all tracked competitors
- [ ] Compare to previous month's scores
- [ ] Screenshot any major changes
- [ ] Check for new citations (Google search: "competitor name" + directory sites)

**Day 4-5:**
- [ ] Test AI queries (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity): "Best [service] in [city]"
- [ ] Document which competitors appear
- [ ] Note any new keywords

### Week 2: Analysis (1 hour)
- [ ] Compare scores month-over-month
- [ ] Identify what changed (use Wayback Machine if needed)
- [ ] Analyze strategy shifts
- [ ] Draft findings

### Week 3: Report Creation (30 min)
- [ ] Fill in Google Docs template
- [ ] Add screenshots/charts
- [ ] Review for accuracy
- [ ] Export to PDF

### Week 4: Delivery & Follow-up (30 min)
- [ ] Send report via email
- [ ] Schedule 15-min review call (optional, for Executive tier)
- [ ] Note any questions/concerns
- [ ] Update tracker for next month
