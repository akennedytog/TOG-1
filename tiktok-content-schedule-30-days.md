## Posting Strategy
- **9am:** Educational/value content (business owners starting day)
- **12pm-3pm:** Quick hits, tips, polls (lunch scrolling)
- **6pm-8pm:** Behind-scenes, personal, results (evening wind-down)
- **Weekends:** Trending audio, lighter content

## Engagement Rules
- Reply to EVERY comment in first 30 minutes
- Pin CTA comment with Calendly link
- Duet/Stitch 3x per week minimum
- Use 5-8 hashtags per post
- Cross-post to Instagram Reels same day

## Content Creation Batch Schedule
- **Sundays 2-4pm:** Film 7 videos for the week
- **Mondays 9am:** Schedule posts in Buffer/Later
- **Daily:** 30 min for engagement/replies
