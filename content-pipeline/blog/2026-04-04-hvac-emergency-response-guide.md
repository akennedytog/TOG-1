## The Emergency Response System

### Step 1: Immediate Answer (0-15 seconds)

**What happens:**
Customer calls your number → AI voice answers → Gathers critical info

**The script:**
```
"Thank you for calling [Company]. This is your 24-hour emergency 
line. I can dispatch a technician right now. 

What's the issue you're experiencing?"

[Listen and categorize]

"Got it. Is this affecting your cooling right now, or is the 
system completely down?"

[Assess urgency]

"I understand. I'm checking technician availability now. 
What's the best callback number?"

[Capture lead]
```

**Why this works:**
- No wait time
- Feels human (natural language)
- Immediately triages urgency
- Captures info while customer is engaged

---

### Step 2: Smart Triage (15-45 seconds)

**AI categorizes the call:**

| Category | Response Time | Action |
|----------|---------------|--------|
| Complete system failure | Immediate | Dispatch on-call tech |
| No cooling (system running) | 2-4 hours | Schedule next available |
| Intermittent issues | Next business day | Book appointment |
| Maintenance request | Next business day | Standard scheduling |

**The intelligence:**
- AC completely down + 90°F outside = Emergency
- AC working but not cooling + 75°F outside = Semi-urgent
- Weird noise but cooling = Standard

---

### Step 3: Instant Dispatch or Scheduling

**For emergencies:**

**What the AI does:**
1. Texts on-call technician with details
2. Sends ETA to customer via SMS
3. Adds job to dispatch calendar
4. Creates customer record in CRM
5. Follows up if tech hasn't confirmed in 10 minutes

**Customer receives:**
```
Technician dispatched: John M.
ETA: 45 minutes
Track arrival: [link]
Questions? Reply to this text.
```

**For non-emergencies:**

**What the AI does:**
1. Offers next 3 available appointment slots
2. Books customer's preferred time
3. Sends confirmation SMS + calendar invite
4. Adds to tech's route optimization
