## Emerging Trends to Watch

### Trend #1: AI Voice Cloning

**What's happening:**
Competitors offering voice cloning for business owners.

**The pitch:**
"Your AI answers with YOUR voice"

**Reality check:**
- Requires extensive training data
- Quality varies significantly
- Regulatory uncertainty

**Our stance:**
Monitoring but not deploying. Focus on reliability over novelty.

---

### Trend #2: All-in-One Platforms

**What's happening:**
Competitors bundling CRM + automation + AI in single platforms.

**The risk:**
- Lock-in effect
- Jack of all trades, master of none
- Higher switching costs

**Our positioning:**
Best-of-breed integration. Use the best tool for each job, integrate seamlessly.

---

### Trend #3: AI Agent Marketplaces

**What's happening:**
OpenAI's GPT Store + custom agent marketplaces.

**The opportunity:**
List our custom agents (Arlo, Dante, Iris) as products?

**Consideration:**
- IP protection
- Support burden
- Brand dilution

**Decision:** Defer until Q3.
