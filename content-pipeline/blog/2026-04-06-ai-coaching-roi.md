## When DIY Makes Sense

**DIY is viable if:**
- You have dedicated technical staff
- Timeline isn't critical
- Budget is truly $0
- Use case is simple and common

**DIY is risky if:**
- You need results in <3 months
- Use case involves customer data
- Integration with existing systems
- Team needs training
