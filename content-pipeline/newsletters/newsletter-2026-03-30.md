### 4. Education

**AI Myth #1:** "You need tons of data to get started"

**Reality:** Most SMBs see results with just 10-20 examples.

The barrier isn't data volume. It's knowing which 10-20 examples actually matter.

Quality of signal > quantity of noise.

### 5. Behind the Scenes

Our AI agent just made its first autonomous decision.

It noticed a pattern in engagement data and suggested we test a new hook format. We approved. We'll see how it performs.

This is the future: AI suggests, humans decide.

### 6. The AI Readiness Test

Ask yourself:
1. Do you repeat this task weekly?
2. Does it follow a pattern you could explain?
3. Would a 10% improvement matter?

If yes to all → You're ready for AI automation

Most businesses have 5-10 tasks that pass this test.

### 7. Strategy

The AI tools that stick share one trait:

They don't ask users to change their workflow. They adapt to it.

The best automation is invisible. It just feels like you're suddenly better at your job.
