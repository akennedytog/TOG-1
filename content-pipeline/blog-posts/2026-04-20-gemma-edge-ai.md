## The News

Google quietly dropped something significant last week: Gemma 4 now runs on iPhone offline. No API calls. No internet required. Just a capable AI model living on your device.

## Why This Matters

### 1. Cost Structure Changes

Cloud AI models charge per token. Every request costs money. For businesses processing thousands of queries daily, these costs add up fast.

Edge AI flips the model:
- **One-time cost:** Download the model
- **Ongoing cost:** Zero
- **Latency:** Near-instant (no network round-trip)

### 2. Privacy by Default

When AI runs locally, your data never leaves the device. For:
- Healthcare applications
- Financial services
- Legal document review
- Any sensitive workflow

This isn't just convenient—it's compliance-friendly.

### 3. Reliability

Cloud services go down. Networks fail. Edge AI works regardless of connectivity. For critical business processes, this matters.

## The Broader Trend

Gemma 4 isn't an isolated release. It's part of a pattern:

- **Apple** running models on-device with Core ML
- **Qualcomm** building AI into mobile chips
- **Microsoft** pushing Copilot to local Windows machines

The shift: AI moving from **cloud-dependent** to **edge-native**.

## What to Expect

1. **More local-first AI tools** - Apps that work without connectivity
2. **Hybrid workflows** - Critical operations on-device, heavy lifting in cloud
3. **New business models** - Sell AI-enabled software, not API access
