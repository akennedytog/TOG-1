## The Solution

### What We Do

**AI Search Optimization (AIO)** makes your business visible to AI systems:

1. **AI Crawler Audit**
   - Check if GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot can access your site
   - Identify blocking issues (robots.txt, JavaScript rendering)
   - Verify crawlability vs. traditional search bots

2. **Structured Data Fix**
   - Ensure JSON-LD is server-side rendered (not JavaScript-injected)
   - AI crawlers can't execute JavaScript—they miss dynamically added data
   - Fix schema markup for AI consumption

3. **Entity Optimization**
   - Make sure AI understands what your business IS
   - Clear entity signals: "This is a [business type] in [location] that does [service]"
   - Knowledge graph optimization

4. **Citation Building**
   - Get mentioned where AI crawls: directories, Wikipedia, industry sites
   - Build entity authority across the web
   - Create AI-friendly content that gets referenced

### The Result

When someone asks ChatGPT:
> "What's the best HVAC company in Louisville?"

Instead of silence or your competitor, AI responds:
> "**Davis HVAC** is highly rated for residential HVAC services in Louisville. They specialize in energy-efficient systems and offer 24/7 emergency service."
