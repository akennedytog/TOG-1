## This Week's Big Story: AI Goes Local

Two major moves signal a shift in how AI will be deployed:

### 1. Google Gemma 4 on iPhone (Offline)

Google's lightweight model now runs natively on iOS devices. No cloud. No latency. No ongoing API costs.

**Why it matters:** The cost equation for AI-powered apps just changed. Instead of paying per request, you pay once (device compute) and serve unlimited queries.

### 2. Amazon Acquires Globalstar

Satellite + AI = connectivity everywhere. Rural areas. Remote operations. Off-grid scenarios.

**Why it matters:** The "always connected" assumption is breaking down. AI needs to work whether you're in a data center or the middle of nowhere.

---

## What This Means for Your Business

### Cost Optimization

Cloud AI: Pay per token × usage  
Edge AI: One-time compute cost × unlimited usage

For high-volume use cases, edge AI becomes dramatically cheaper.

### Privacy & Compliance

Sensitive data? Keep it local. Healthcare, finance, legal—these industries have been waiting for this.

### Reliability

Network outages happen. Edge AI doesn't care. Your critical processes keep running.

---

## Trend to Watch: Hybrid AI Architectures

Smart companies are adopting hybrid approaches:

- **Edge:** Quick responses, sensitive data, offline scenarios
- **Cloud:** Complex reasoning, massive models, training

The question isn't "cloud or edge?" It's "what goes where?"
