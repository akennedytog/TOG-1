The Problem: Lead Response Bottleneck

Davis HVAC is a 12-person residential HVAC company in Kentucky. Like most service businesses, they live and die by lead response speed. But their process was broken:

  • Leads came in from website, phone, and Google Ads
  • Owner John personally handled all follow-ups
  • Average response time: 2-4 hours (sometimes next day)
  • 60% of leads never got a second touch
  • 2 hours/day spent on follow-up tasks

John knew speed mattered. He'd read the studies: responding within 5 minutes makes you 100x more likely to connect. But he was also running the business, managing techs, and handling emergencies. Something had to give—and it was usually follow-up.

The Solution: AI Lead Response Agent

We deployed a simple AI system that:

  • Responds to inquiries in under 60 seconds with a personalized message
  • Qualifies leads by asking about square footage, system type, and urgency
  • Schedules appointments directly into their calendar
  • Follows up automatically with unresponsive leads after 24 hours
  • Flags hot leads for John to personally call

The AI was trained on 20 of John's best email responses. It learned his tone—professional but friendly, direct but not pushy. It knew their service areas, pricing tiers, and scheduling constraints.

The Results

Week 1: The AI handled 47 leads. John reviewed them all and made 3 tweaks to the responses. Response time dropped from 2+ hours to under 60 seconds.

Week 4: Response rate improved 40%. More leads were answering because they were getting immediate responses—even at 11 PM on a Saturday.

Week 8: Meetings booked tripled. The AI was qualifying leads better than John had time to. It asked all the right questions upfront, so their techs arrived prepared.

Week 12: John was spending 15 minutes/day reviewing AI-handled leads instead of 2 hours on follow-up. He used that time to train new techs and expand service areas.

"The AI didn't replace our team—it gave them superpowers. They're closing more deals and actually have time to breathe. I wish I'd done this two years ago."
— John Davis, Owner, Davis HVAC

The Real Win

It's not just about saving 10 hours/week. It's about what John does with those 10 hours. He's building the business instead of chasing leads. He's training staff instead of answering emails. He's sleeping better instead of worrying about missed opportunities.

That's the ROI that matters.
