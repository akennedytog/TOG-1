## GLOSSARY

**AI Crawler:** Automated programs (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) that read websites to train AI models.

**Structured Data:** Code (JSON-LD) that tells AI exactly what your business is, where it is, and what it does.

**Entity Recognition:** AI's ability to understand "This is a [business type] in [location]."

**Citation:** Any mention of your business on another website (directories, reviews, articles).

**JSON-LD:** JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data—a way to structure information for machines.

**Schema.org:** Standard vocabulary for structured data markup.
