# 7-Day Content Strategy - The One Group

## Framework (Daily)

### Morning Post (9am) - Observation/Learning
- Real discovery from previous day
- Something you noticed about AI/OpenClaw
- Pattern or trend worth sharing

### Afternoon Post (3pm) - Question/Engagement  
- Ask the audience something
- Poll-style content
- "What do you think about X?"

### Evening Post (9pm) - Behind Scenes/Story
- Mission Control progress
- Something you built/tried
- Lesson learned

---

## Template A (Any Day)

**Morning:** Started playing with OpenClaw's new browser-use skill yesterday. Most interesting part isn't what it CAN do - it's what you suddenly don't have to remember to check anymore. What mundane task would you automate first?

**Afternoon:** Honest question: What's the most overhyped AI feature right now? I'll start - AI image generation for business use cases. Cool demo, rarely practical.

**Evening:** Just finished wiring my 8th agent into Mission Control. Took 3 attempts. The first two failed because I assumed the config syntax. Third time I just copy-pasted from the working one and tweaked. Sometimes the docs lie. Ship anyway.

---

## Template B (Any Day)

**Morning:** Noticed something this week: The people actually shipping AI stuff aren't the ones posting daily "AI will replace X" threads. They're heads down building. Quietest builders = loudest results?

**Afternoon:** Quick poll: When you hear "AI automation" what's your first thought? A) Exciting B) Scary C) Buzzword D) Tell me more

**Evening:** Weekend wrap: This week I learned that agent coordination is harder than agent creation. Building 8 agents was easy. Making them talk to each other? That's where it gets interesting. Mission Control screenshot below. What should agent #9 do?

---

## Template C (Weekend)

**Morning:** Weekend thought: The best AI tools aren't the ones with the most features. They're the ones that disappear into your workflow. You stop noticing they're AI and just use them. What's a tool that crossed that threshold for you?

**Afternoon:** Unpopular opinion: Most businesses don't need AI strategy. They need "stop doing manual stuff" strategy. AI happens to be the current answer. The problem is older than the tech.

**Evening:** Weekend experiment: Asked 5 friends what they think I actually do. Got 5 different answers. Clear sign I need to work on my elevator pitch. Or embrace the confusion. How do YOU explain your work?

---

## Template D (Weekend Reflection)

**Morning:** Weekend reflection: Three weeks ago I was manually posting to Twitter. Now I have an agent (Dante) creating content, another (Rico) scheduling it, and a dashboard showing me what's queued. The change isn't the output - it's the mental bandwidth I got back.

**Afternoon:** If you could automate one thing about your weekend routine, what would it be? Me: The "I should prep for next week" anxiety.

**Evening:** Week in review: Posted consistently. Generated leads. Built new features in Mission Control. Slept... less than I should have. But the system is working. What did you ship this week?

---

## Template E (Start of Week)

**Morning:** Insight: The gap between "I could automate this" and "I did automate this" is usually 20 minutes of setup. The blocker isn't complexity. It's perceived complexity. What are you procrastinating on that might be easier than you think?

**Afternoon:** Check-in: What's the one thing you want to get done this week? Drop it below. Accountability thread.

**Evening:** Reality: Planned to build X, spent 4 hours debugging Y, ended up learning Z. Classic. The detours usually teach more than the destination. What did you accidentally learn today?

---

## Template F (Mid-Week)

**Morning:** Observation: The most useful AI features are boring. "Summarize this email" isn't sexy. But it saves 10 minutes 10 times a day. That's 100 minutes. What's your most boring-but-valuable AI use case?

**Afternoon:** If AI could handle ONE part of your job perfectly, which would you choose? A) Email B) Scheduling C) Research D) Reporting E) Something else (tell me)

**Evening:** Progress check: Mission Control now shows real-time agent status. Watching the system work feels like having a tiny team that never sleeps. Also never complains. What would your 8-agent team do?

---

## Template G (Mid-Week Reflection)

**Morning:** Realization: Been using OpenClaw for a while. Still discovering features. The depth is wild. What tool are you using that's probably 10x more powerful than you're currently using?

**Afternoon:** Mid-week check: How's your energy? Scale of 1-10. If it's low, what's draining you? If it's high, what's fueling you? Trying to understand the patterns.

**Evening:** Experiment update: Daily posts, decent engagement, learning every day. The real metric I'm tracking? How much I enjoy doing it. What's your sustainability metric?

## Daily Trend Monitoring

### Sources to Check Daily (5 min each morning)
1. Hacker News - AI/Automation trending
2. Reddit r/OpenClaw - community questions
3. Twitter trending - #OpenClaw #AI #Automation
4. OpenAI blog - new features/releases
5. TechCrunch AI section - industry moves

### Adaptation Rules
- If major OpenClaw update → pivot to feature explanation
- If trending AI topic → add hot take/observation
- If community asking same question → create educational post
- If personal breakthrough → share behind scenes

---

## Content Refresh Rules

**Every Morning (8am):**
1. Check trending topics
2. Review yesterday's engagement
3. Adjust today's posts if needed
4. Generate tomorrow's posts based on patterns

**Replace content if:**
- Something major happened in AI world
- Yesterday's post tanked (learn from it)
- Better idea emerged overnight
- Current events make scheduled post tone-deaf
