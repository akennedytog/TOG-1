|------|------------|-----|
| **Meme formats** | High shares | Visual humor, relatable |
| **AI failures** | High replies | Everyone has stories |
| **POV format** | High engagement | Direct, personal |
| **Hot takes** | High quote tweets | Contrarian = discussion |
| **Storytelling** | Medium-high | Authentic, human |

### ❌ What's NOT in here
- No sales pitches
- No product mentions
- No "DM me for details"
- No webinars/lead magnets
- No "book a call"

### 🎯 Success Metrics
- **Primary:** Replies (engagement)
- **Secondary:** Shares (reach)
- **Tertiary:** Profile visits (traffic)

### 📊 Expectation
Fun content builds brand awareness, which drives profile visits, which leads to discovery of business content.

**Not expecting direct leads from these.**
