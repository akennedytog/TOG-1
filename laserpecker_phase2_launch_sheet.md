## Production Queue (First 30 Days)
1. Week 1: List PT-01, PT-02, TB-01, TB-06, CB-02 (fast review velocity).
2. Week 2: Add TB-02, TB-04, CB-01, WD-01, WD-02.
3. Week 3: Add TB-03, TB-05, CB-03, CB-04, PT-03.
4. Week 4: Double down only on top 5 conversion SKUs with better photos, short videos, and price tests (+/-$2).

## Daily Ops Targets
- Publish 1 listing update/day (photo, title, or first-line description test).
- Message response SLA under 2 hours during business hours.
- 48-hour rush processing add-on on every listing.
- Push average order value above $40 with bundles and add-ons.

## Exact Offer Stack (Use On Every Listing)
- `Base product`: the listed SKU at core price.
- `Rush add-on`: +$12 to +$20 for 48-hour processing.
- `Gift box add-on`: +$6 to +$10 for premium presentation.
- `Proof add-on`: free first proof, $5 each extra revision after 2 rounds.
- `Bundle upsell`: suggest matching item in listing images and FAQ.

## 14-Day Execution Sprint
1. Day 1: Finalize material blanks for PT-01, PT-02, TB-01, TB-06, CB-02 and produce 3 samples each.
2. Day 2: Shoot listing photos (8 photos + 1 short video per SKU), including close-up of engraving quality.
3. Day 3: Publish first 5 Etsy listings with personalization fields and shop policies.
4. Day 4: Build message templates (proof approval, rush fee, shipping delay, thank-you).
5. Day 5: Launch Etsy Ads at $5/day only on PT-01 and TB-01.
6. Day 6: Create 2 social proof clips (engraving process + packaged order) and post to TikTok/Instagram.
7. Day 7: Run first pricing test (+$2 on best click-through listing).
8. Day 8: Publish second batch (TB-02, TB-04, CB-01, WD-01, WD-02).
9. Day 9: Contact 20 local leads (realtors, wedding planners, pet groomers) with sample photos.
10. Day 10: Add FAQ and care cards to every listing and package.
11. Day 11: Publish final batch (TB-03, TB-05, CB-03, CB-04, PT-03).
12. Day 12: Review conversion by SKU and pause any listing below 1% conversion.
13. Day 13: Re-shoot weak thumbnails and rewrite first 140 chars of low-performing titles.
14. Day 14: Keep only top 5 SKUs in ads, raise prices by $2-$4 where conversion remains stable.

## Weekly KPI Targets (First 60 Days)
- `Conversion rate`: 1.8%+ shopwide, 2.5%+ on top SKUs.
- `Average order value`: $40+ by week 4, $48+ by week 8.
- `Gross margin`: 48%+ mixed basket, 55%+ on pet tags.
- `On-time ship rate`: 98%+.
- `Review velocity`: 12+ new reviews per month.

## What To Make With LP4 First
- IR + rotary: all tumblers and pet tags first for speed and consistency.
- Diode: boards and wedding wood tags when personalization workflow is dialed in.
- Keep one material family per day to reduce setup waste and mistakes.
