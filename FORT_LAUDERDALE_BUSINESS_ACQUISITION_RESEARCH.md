## 💰 FINANCING OPTIONS

### SBA 7(a) Loan
- Up to $5M
- 10% down typical
- 10-year term
- Requires personal guarantee

### Seller Financing
- 10-30% of purchase price
- 3-5 year terms common
- Shows seller confidence

### Asset-Based Lending
- Use equipment/inventory as collateral
- Higher rates but faster

### Earnout Structure
- Lower upfront payment
- Performance-based additional payments
- Aligns incentives

## ⚠️ RED FLAGS TO AVOID

1. **Declining revenue** - "Market is changing" = run
2. **Owner does everything** - Can't automate if business = owner
3. **Undocumented processes** - Everything in owner's head
4. **High customer concentration** - 1-2 clients = 50%+ revenue
5. **No recurring revenue** - One-time sales are hard to automate
6. **Unlicensed/uninsured** - Legal liability
7. **Family employees** - Drama, unclear roles
8. **Cash business** - Can't verify income
9. **New competition entering** - Market share at risk
10. **Owner staying on** - "Transition period" = never leaves
