Questions & Concerns

Is this defamation?

We require verification to reduce fake reviews. Ratings must be based on actual work experience. Legal protections and moderation systems will be implemented before launch.

What if I get unfairly rated?

Multiple ratings average out. One bad review won't define you. The 5-category system (Executive, High Performer, Average, Below Average, Needs Improvement) is more objective than free-form comments.

Can my employer find out?

Your identity is anonymous. We use company email only for verification, not display. Advanced privacy protections prevent deanonymization.

Why no written reviews?

No comments means no defamation risk. The skill voting system (up/down votes) gives constructive feedback without the toxicity of written reviews. Star ratings + 5 categories + skill votes = all the signal, none of the noise.

What stops fake ratings?

Verification required. Time-delayed ratings. Pattern detection algorithms. Rate limiting. Multiple safeguards against gaming.
