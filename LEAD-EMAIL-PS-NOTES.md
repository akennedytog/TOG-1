**Purpose:** Broaden positioning. The current pitch is all missed-calls / lead-response AI.
This PS invites the lead to bring **ANY** AI need and positions The One Group as a full AI partner.
Keep it in every email. Pick the industry variant that matches the lead.

> **P.S.** And honestly — the missed-call thing is just the tip of the iceberg. If you've got *any* AI problem eating your time or your margins, bring it to me. I'll tell you straight whether it's worth automating, what it'll cost, and what it'll save you. No pitch, no pressure — just a straight answer. Reply with whatever's bugging you.

## Why this hits harder
- **"Tip of the iceberg"** — reframes the missed-call pitch as one symptom of a bigger problem, not the whole offer.
- **Industry-specific pain** — names the exact thing that hurts *that* business (billable hours, no-shows, cold leads, margins), so it reads like it was written for them, not blasted.
- **"I'll tell you straight"** — positions you as a trusted advisor, not a salesperson. Lowers the guard.
- **"No pitch, no pressure"** — removes the fear of getting sold to, which is the #1 reason leads don't reply.
- **"Bring it to me"** — opens the door to *any* AI need, exactly the broadening you asked for.
