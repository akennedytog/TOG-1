# South Florida SBA 7(a) Lenders & Brokers — Business Acquisition

**Prepared for:** Alec Kennedy | **Date:** August 30, 2026
**Goal:** Pre-qualify for an SBA 7(a) loan to buy a cash-flowing South Florida business.

---

## How to Use This List

Get **2–3 quotes minimum** — one national acquisition specialist + one local/regional lender. The national specialist confirms the deal type is financeable and gives a competitive benchmark; the local lender gives speed, regional nuance, and a banking relationship after closing.

**Key filter:** Look for **Preferred Lender Program (PLP)** status — it lets the bank approve SBA loans in-house instead of waiting on the SBA, often saving weeks.

---

## National Acquisition Specialists (best for deals $1M+)

### 1. Live Oak Bank — #1 SBA 7(a) lender in the country
- **Why:** ~$2.8B in approvals last fiscal year, ~14% of all acquisition loan dollars nationally (more than any other bank). Branchless, industry-specialized, underwrites business purchases daily, comfortable with goodwill-heavy deals.
- **Best for:** Acquisitions $1M+, goodwill-heavy deals.
- **Trade-off:** National machine, no local branch; your file competes for attention.
- **Action:** Should almost always be one of your quotes for deals above ~$1M.

### 2. Newtek Bank — #2 SBA 7(a) lender
- **Why:** $2B+ in annual approvals. Tech-driven, high-volume, lends nationally including Florida, willing to look at deals other banks pass on.
- **Best for:** Smaller and harder-to-place deals.
- **Trade-off:** Process-driven, less hand-holding.

### 3. Huntington National Bank — top-3 SBA volume
- **Why:** Consistently first/near-first in number of SBA loans written; built for main-street deal sizes.
- **Best for:** Main-street deal sizes.
- **Trade-off:** Midwest-centered footprint; Florida borrowers work with its national SBA group remotely.

## What to Have Ready Before You Call

- 2 years personal tax returns
- 2 years wife's LLC tax returns (needs 2 yrs consistency to count)
- 2 years bank statements
- Personal financial statement (PFS)
- Target business profile (SDE, price, industry) — see the Target Profile doc
- Your equity source plan (see the Oct 1 rule — at least half your 10% down must be your own cash)
