## Engagement Hooks

### Polls That Get Replies:

**Finance Literacy Check:**
```
Quick check:

Without looking it up, do you know:

A) Your current runway (months of cash)
B) Your gross margin by product/service
C) Your customer acquisition cost
D) Your lifetime value per customer
E) All of the above
F) I have no idea what any of these mean

No judgment. Just curious where people are.
```

**Tool Adoption:**
```
What's your finance tool situation?

A) Excel/spreadsheets only
B) QuickBooks + manual work
C) Modern accounting + some automation
D) Full AI-assisted finance stack
E) Accountant handles everything
F) Chaos and prayers

What's working? What's broken?
```

**Pain Point:**
```
What's your biggest finance workflow pain?

A) Receipt/expense tracking
B) Invoice chasing
C) Month-end close
D) Forecasting (guessing the future)
E) Understanding what the numbers mean
F) Finding time to do any of this

The answer you pick says a lot about what to automate first.
```

## Success Metrics

Track these for finance content:

1. **Bookmark rate** - Are people saving this for reference?
2. **Reply depth** - Are people sharing their own experiences?
3. **DM rate** - Are people asking for specific recommendations?
4. **Quote-tweet rate** - Are people sharing with their own commentary?

Finance content should get FEWER likes but MORE saves/replies than generic AI content.
