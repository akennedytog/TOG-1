# LaserPecker LP4 Phase 3 - B2B Outreach Scripts

## Offer Positioning
- Core promise: `Personalized gifts that help local businesses create memorable client moments and repeat referrals.`
- Speed edge: `Local production + 48-hour rush capability.`
- Margin edge for partner: `They upsell personalization without owning production.`

## Script 1 - Realtor / Mortgage Email
Subject: Closing gift idea your clients will keep forever

Hi {{name}},

I run a local engraving studio in Louisville and we make personalized closing gifts (boards, tumblers, gift bundles) that agents/lenders use to make move-in day memorable.

Why it works:
- Better client experience at close
- More social shares and referral mentions
- Fast local turnaround, including rush jobs

I can send a free mockup with your logo and client name style this week.

Want me to mock up 2 options for your team?

- Alec

## Script 2 - Wedding Planner / Venue Email
Subject: Personalized wedding favors your couples will actually keep

Hi {{name}},

I create custom laser-engraved wedding items locally (place tags, bridal mirrors, wedding party gifts) and can support planner/venue partners with quick-turn production.

Would you be open to a partner menu for your couples with preferred pricing and rush options?

I can send a mini lookbook with your brand on it.

- Alec

## Script 3 - Pet Groomer / Pet Shop DM
Hi {{name}}! I’m local and make engraved pet ID tags with 48-hour turnaround. 

A lot of groomers add tags at checkout as a simple, high-margin upsell. If you want, I can drop off sample tags and set up a starter wholesale pack.

Worth a quick chat this week?

Open to that?

## Script 5 - Call Opener (30 seconds)
Hi, this is Alec. I run a local laser engraving studio and I’m calling because we help {{segment}} teams offer personalized gifts without adding production work on your side. 

Would you be the right person to speak with about partner gift options?

## Objection Handles
- "We already have a vendor": No problem. I can be your overflow/rush partner so you never miss deadlines.
- "No budget": Start with a tiny pilot run and use only on premium clients/events.
- "Not sure it fits": I can provide mockups using your brand and one sample order format.

## Weekly Outreach Cadence
1. Monday: 8 new prospect emails + 4 DMs.
2. Tuesday: 6 follow-up calls.
3. Wednesday: Deliver sample mockups to warm leads.
4. Thursday: Close 1 pilot order.
5. Friday: Ask pilot buyers for testimonial and referral.
