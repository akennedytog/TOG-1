## Why This Pillar (and how it's different)

**The edge:** Most AI-investing accounts are hype/pump-driven. Very few credible voices cover
the **intersection** of AI and space. You're invested in both AND you run an AI automation
agency — so you can analyze these sectors through an **operator's lens** (unit economics,
adoption curves, capex, real business sense) instead of price-target guessing.

**Positioning rule — do NOT be a financial advisor:**
- ✅ Analyze the *business* of AI/space: economics, buildouts, adoption, margins
- ✅ Talk as an investor with skin in the game
- ❌ Give price targets, "buy/sell" calls, or act as financial advice (compliance + credibility)

**⚡ NO SELF-PROMO RULE (2026-08-20):** NEVER mention Alec's AI automation agency in tweets.
No "I run an automation agency", no "as an AI-automation operator", no agency cred. It reads cocky and
undercuts the investor-voice credibility. Position purely as an investor. (Tweet 3 "operator lens" was
posted before this rule and includes an agency reference — do NOT repeat that framing.)

**Your moat:** "An operator who runs AI automation for SMBs AND is a long in AI/space."
Nobody sounds like that. It reads authentic, not clickbait.

## Tone & Guardrails
- **Operator first, investor second.** Lead with *business logic*, not price.
- **Contrarian but factual** — challenge consensus with reasoning, not hot takes.
- **Acknowledge risk** — space stocks are volatile (ASTS/RKLB swing hard). Saying
  "this is a long-term buildout, not a smooth ride" builds more trust than hype.
- **Disclose holdings naturally** — "I'm long both" adds credibility; never pretend otherwise.
- **Include $tickers + $cashtag** for discoverability.

## Seed Play: $KEEL + $HIVE (the "AI compute buildout" pair)

**Thesis:** Two different layers of the same AI data-center buildout.

- **$HIVE (HIVE Digital)** — a Bitcoin miner pivoting its data centers into
  GPU/AI-HPC compute. Play on *who runs the GPUs*.
- **$KEEL (Keel Infrastructure)** — data infrastructure & analytics provider.
  Play on *the physical/logical data layer under* the compute.

**Why "both" (the tweet angle):** If you believe the AI buildout is real, you can own it
at two levels — the compute repurposers (HIVE) and the infrastructure layer (KEEL).
It's a barbell across the same trend, not two random speculative bets.

**Guardian:** both are small-caps (KEEL ~$3.37, HIVE ~$3.04) with wide 52-week ranges.
Frame as "long-term infrastructure buildout" and be honest about volatility.
