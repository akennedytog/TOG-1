SUBJECT: Not selling - just sharing what worked for a Miami HVAC company

Hey everyone,

Not here to pitch. Just wanted to share something interesting I saw working with an HVAC contractor in South Florida.

Their problem: They were missing 12-15 calls a week during busy season. Customers calling after hours, during jobs, when everyone's on calls already. Standard stuff.

They tried:
- Hiring a part-time receptionist ($800/week, couldn't keep up)
- Call answering service ($3/call, but leads went cold waiting for callback)

What worked: AI phone answering that books directly into their calendar

Results after 30 days:
- Missed calls: 15/week → 3/week
- Appointments booked: +18 (from calls that would've gone to voicemail)
- No-shows: Down 40% (because they get SMS confirmations)

The AI isn't magic - it's just consistent. Answers 24/7, qualifies the lead ("emergency or quote?"), checks availability, books the slot.

Biggest surprise: Customers didn't care it wasn't human. They cared that someone answered and they got an appointment.

If anyone's curious about the setup (not selling, just geeking out on the tech), happy to share.
