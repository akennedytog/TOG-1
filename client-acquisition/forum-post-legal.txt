SUBJECT: Crazy stat from a law firm I worked with

Quick story for the group:

A 5-attorney firm in Fort Lauderdale was losing an average of 8 potential client calls per day to voicemail.

Not because they were bad at their jobs. Because:
- They're in court 9-3
- Existing clients call with "emergencies" 
- Receptionist was overwhelmed

They tracked it for a week: 47 calls went to voicemail. 31 never called back.

That's potentially 31 new cases. Gone.

They set up an AI intake system that:
1. Answers immediately ("Hi, this is [Firm], are you calling about a new case or existing client?")
2. For new cases: Collects basic info, schedules consultation
3. For existing clients: Takes message, sends to attorney via SMS

30 days later:
- Missed calls: 47/week → 8/week
- New consultations scheduled: +22/month
- Existing clients: Actually get faster responses (AI texts attorney immediately)

The attorney told me: "I thought this would feel impersonal. Clients literally don't care. They just want their problem solved."

Anyway - if anyone's dealing with the "phone tag" problem, this setup cost them $297/month vs the $4,200/month they were spending on a full-time intake person who was burning out.

Not selling anything. Just thought it was a cool use case.
