## Voice Message Tips:

1. **Keep it under 45 seconds**
2. **Say their name twice** (gets attention)
3. **End with an easy out** ("either way..." "if not, totally get it")
4. **One ask only** - don't ask for call + email + meeting
5. **Send between 9-11am or 2-4pm** (highest response rates)
