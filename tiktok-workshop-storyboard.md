# TikTok Video Storyboard

## Frame-by-Frame Breakdown

---

### SCENE 1: Hook (0-3 sec)
**Shot:** Face-to-camera OR screen recording
**Text Overlay:** "I'm showing you my entire AI setup. For free."
**Effect:** Fast zoom in on "free"

---

### SCENE 2: The Agents (3-8 sec)
**Shot:** Screen recording - Mission Control dashboard
**Text Overlay:** 
- "8 AI Agents"
- "24/7 operation"
**Visual:** Cursor moving between agent panels

---

### SCENE 3: What They Do (8-15 sec)
**Shot:** Split screen or quick cuts
**Text Overlay:**
- Arlo → "Finds leads"
- Dante → "Writes content"  
- Iris → "Sends emails"
**Effect:** Each agent name pops up with their icon

---

### SCENE 4: Live Proof (15-22 sec)
**Shot:** Screen recording with real numbers
**Text Overlay:**
- "514 leads this month"
- "75 posts generated"
- "23 outreach emails"
**Effect:** Numbers counting up animation

---

### SCENE 5: Workshop Details (22-28 sec)
**Shot:** Calendar graphic OR you holding up fingers
**Text Overlay:**
- "May 20th"
- "5:30 PM EST"
- "20 spots only"
**Effect:** Calendar flips to May 20

---

### SCENE 6: CTA (28-35 sec)
**Shot:** Back to face-to-camera OR pointing at screen
**Text Overlay:** 
- "Link in bio"
- URL displayed
**Effect:** Arrow pointing down to bio area

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Vertical format** - 1080x1920
2. **Good lighting** - Face the window
3. **Clear audio** - Use AirPods if no mic
4. **Hook in first 1 second** - Start with movement
5. **Captions ON** - 80% watch without sound

---

## TikTok-Specific Features to Use

- **Green Screen** - Put yourself in front of dashboard screenshots
- **Text-to-Speech** - If you don't want to narrate
- **Stitch** - Invite people to ask questions first
- **Duet** - Have past attendees share their experience
- **Poll** - "Want to see how X works?" in comments
