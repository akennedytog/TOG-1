## 💡 PRO TIPS

### When Calling:
1. **Mention you're local** — brokers prefer local buyers (easier to close)
2. **Say "pre-qualified"** — separates you from tire-kickers
3. **Be specific** — "$400-600K" not "I'm looking at stuff"
4. **Ask for off-market** — best deals never list publicly

### In Emails:
1. **Keep it short** — brokers are busy
2. **Lead with numbers** — price range, cash flow target
3. **Show you're serious** — mention SBA pre-qualification
4. **Follow up** — 50% of responses come from follow-ups

### On LinkedIn:
1. **Connect first** — don't pitch in connection request
2. **Reference their listings** — shows you did homework
3. **Be professional** — this is a relationship business
