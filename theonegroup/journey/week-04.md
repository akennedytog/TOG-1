## What is the Beta Access System?

A simple but effective way to onboard our first 10 users:

### Components

1. **Landing Page** (`theonegroup-site/`)
   - Clear value proposition: "AI automation that actually works"
   - Social proof: Twitter growth, engagement metrics
   - Simple signup form (name, email, business type, biggest pain point)
   - FAQ addressing common concerns

2. **Waitlist Management** (Airtable/Notion/Sheet)
   - Track signups
   - Segment by use case
   - Prioritize by fit (SMBs with clear automation needs)

3. **Onboarding Flow**
   - Welcome email with next steps
   - 15-min discovery call (qualify + understand needs)
   - Custom automation proposal
   - 2-week trial period
   - Feedback collection

4. **Feedback Loop**
   - Weekly check-ins
   - Usage analytics
   - Success metrics tracking
   - Case study permission

### Success Criteria for Beta

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Signups | 50+ (select 10) |
| Activation | 8/10 complete onboarding |
| Retention | 7/10 still active after 2 weeks |
| NPS | > 50 |
| Case studies | 3+ willing to share results |
