# Offer Stack

## Positioning
The One Group helps service businesses win AI search visibility and convert more of the traffic they already have.

## Ladder

### 1) AI Visibility Sprint (front-end)
- Price: $1,500 fixed
- Timeline: 5 business days
- Tools: `open-seo` + manual analysis
- Deliverables:
  - Site visibility and technical audit
  - Competitor benchmark (3 competitors)
  - Priority fixes ranked by impact
  - 30-day action plan
- CTA: "Want us to implement this for you in 2 weeks?"

### 2) AI Growth Implementation (core project)
- Price: $4,000 to $8,000
- Timeline: 2 to 4 weeks
- Tools: `chrome-devtools-mcp`, engineering + copy updates
- Deliverables:
  - Conversion and technical fixes
  - Schema, content entity clarity, and internal linking fixes
  - Offer-page optimization and CTA rewrite
  - Tracking setup for lead attribution
- CTA: "Want monthly optimization and monitoring so gains compound?"

### 3) AI Growth Ops Retainer (recurring)
- Price: $1,500 to $3,000 per month
- Term: 3-month minimum
- Tools: `langflow` + reporting + continuous optimization
- Deliverables:
  - Monthly visibility and conversion optimization
  - Competitor movement watchlist and response actions
  - Automation flows for lead qualification and follow-up
  - Monthly strategy call + KPI report

## Optional Add-ons
- Competitor Intel Lite: $497/month
- Conversion Teardown: $750 one-time
- Team AI Playbook Workshop: $1,200 half day

## Packaging Rules
- Do not sell retainers cold.
- Sell Sprint first to prove competence quickly.
- Use implementation scope from sprint findings, not generic bundles.
- Keep statement of work tied to measurable business outcomes.

## Outcome Language
- Better qualified leads
- Higher close rate from current traffic
- Faster follow-up and lower lead leakage
- Reduced dependence on ad spend for growth
