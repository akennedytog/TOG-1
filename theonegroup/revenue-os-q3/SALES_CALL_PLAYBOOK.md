# Sales Call Playbook

## Call Structure (30 minutes)
1. Context and goal (3 min)
2. Current pipeline reality (7 min)
3. Diagnose leakage points (8 min)
4. Confirm impact and urgency (6 min)
5. Recommend next step and close (6 min)

## Discovery Questions
- Where do your best leads come from today?
- What percent of inquiries become booked calls?
- What happens between first inquiry and first human touch?
- Where do you think you are losing deals right now?
- What would a 20 percent lift in qualified inquiries be worth monthly?

## Diagnosis Framework
- Visibility: are they discoverable in high-intent channels?
- Conversion: does the site convert intent into action?
- Follow-up: do leads get contacted fast and consistently?
- Proof: does the site reduce buyer uncertainty?

## Transition to Offer
"Based on what you shared, I would not jump to a long retainer yet. The fastest path is a 5-day AI Visibility Sprint so we can prioritize the exact fixes and ROI."

## Pricing Anchor
- Sprint: $1,500
- If they proceed to implementation within 14 days, credit $500 from sprint fee.

## Objection Handling

### "We need to think about it"
"Makes sense. Is the hesitation priority, budget, or confidence in outcome?"

### "Too expensive"
"Totally fair. Compared with one month of underperforming paid traffic or missed inbound deals, this is usually the lower-cost move."

### "Can you just send a proposal"
"Yes. I will send it today. To keep it accurate, I need one metric: average monthly qualified inquiries."

## Close Options
- Option A: pay and start sprint this week
- Option B: schedule a paid strategy workshop if not ready for sprint
- Option C: close-lost with clear reactivation date

## Post-Call SLA
- Proposal sent within 2 hours
- Follow-up touch at 48 hours
- Decision checkpoint by Day 7
