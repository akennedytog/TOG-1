# Delivery Playbook

## Tool-to-Outcome Mapping

### open-seo
Use for:
- technical visibility baseline
- page-level issue discovery
- competitor benchmark snapshots
Outcome:
- ranked backlog of high-impact fixes

### chrome-devtools-mcp
Use for:
- page speed and runtime debugging
- form flow and CTA friction checks
- mobile interaction quality
Outcome:
- conversion uplift and reduced drop-off

### langflow
Use for:
- lead qualification flow design
- follow-up automation logic
- internal handoff workflows
Outcome:
- faster response time and less lead leakage

### codebase-memory-mcp
Use for:
- faster codebase onboarding
- implementation context persistence
- handoff continuity and QA
Outcome:
- reduced delivery time and fewer regressions

## Sprint SOP (5 days)
- Day 1: kickoff, access, baseline crawl
- Day 2: issue classification and competitor benchmark
- Day 3: prioritization and ROI estimate
- Day 4: report and implementation scope
- Day 5: review call and implementation decision

## Implementation SOP (2 to 4 weeks)
- Week 1: technical and conversion quick wins
- Week 2: schema/content/entity fixes + measurement
- Week 3+: automation flows and QA
- End: before/after report and retainer recommendation

## Retainer SOP (monthly)
- Week 1: priorities and experiments
- Week 2: implementation sprint
- Week 3: reporting and competitor watch
- Week 4: strategy call and next-month plan

## Client Update Rhythm
- 2 written updates per week
- same-day response SLA for blocker questions
- monthly executive summary with KPI deltas

## Minimum KPI Set
- qualified inquiries
- booked calls
- inquiry-to-call conversion rate
- average response time to new lead
- monthly pipeline value influenced
