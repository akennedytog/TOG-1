# 30-Day Launch Plan

## Week 1 - Foundation and Pipeline
- Finalize offer page copy and outbound scripts
- Build list of 150 ICP targets across 2 verticals
- Send first 100 touches
- Book 5 discovery calls
- Deliver 2 micro-teardowns publicly for authority

## Week 2 - Calls and First Wins
- Send next 120 touches
- Run 8 to 10 discovery calls
- Close first 2 AI Visibility Sprints
- Start delivery immediately and collect early proof
- Publish one case-style post with concrete findings

## Week 3 - Convert to Implementation
- Deliver sprint reports for Week 2 clients
- Pitch implementation with a 14-day credit window
- Close 1 to 2 implementation projects
- Continue outbound at 40 touches per day
- Build referral ask into every client touchpoint

## Week 4 - Retainer Conversion and Systemization
- Complete implementation milestones
- Present before/after impact snapshots
- Close 1 to 2 retainers
- Document repeatable SOP updates
- Review KPI scoreboard and reset targets for next month

## Weekly Scorecard Targets
- New touches: 200
- Replies: 20+
- Discovery calls: 5+
- Sprints sold: 2+
- Implementation sold: 1+
- Retainers sold: 0 to 1 by Week 3, 1+ by Week 4

## Daily Schedule (example)
- 8:00 to 9:00 list building and personalization
- 9:00 to 10:30 outbound block
- 11:00 to 1:00 calls and follow-ups
- 2:00 to 5:00 delivery and proposals
- 5:00 to 5:30 KPI update and next-day prep
