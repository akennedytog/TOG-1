# Outbound Playbook

## Daily Activity Targets
- 20 cold emails
- 10 warm DMs
- 10 follow-ups
- 3 loom-style quick audit notes sent

## List Criteria
- Decision maker name known
- Revenue fit and service vertical fit
- Website active and conversion friction visible
- Local market with visible competition

## Cold Email Template 1 (short)
Subject: quick win for {{company}}

{{first_name}} - quick note.
I checked {{company}} and found 3 fixable issues likely costing qualified leads.

I can send a 1-page breakdown with priority fixes.
No pitch deck, just what to do first.

Worth sending?

- Alec

## Cold Email Template 2 (with observation)
Subject: {{company}} visibility gap

{{first_name}},
I noticed {{specific issue}} on {{company}}'s site while checking local AI/search visibility in {{city}}.

I run a 5-day AI Visibility Sprint for service businesses:
- technical and entity visibility findings
- competitor benchmark
- ranked fixes by impact

If useful, I can send a free 3-point teardown first.

- Alec

## Follow-up Sequence
- Day 2: "Want me to send the 3-point teardown?"
- Day 5: include one concrete issue screenshot
- Day 9: "close the loop" message
- Day 14: final break-up, ask for referral internally

## DM Script (LinkedIn/X)
Hey {{name}} - I reviewed {{company}} and found a few visibility and conversion leaks. If helpful, I can send a short teardown with top 3 fixes and expected impact.

## Call Booking Script
"If this is relevant, we can do a 20-minute working call and I will map your top 5 fixes live."

## Rules
- Keep messages under 120 words.
- Mention one specific observation.
- One CTA only.
- No attachments on first touch.
