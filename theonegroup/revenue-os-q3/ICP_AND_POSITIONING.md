# ICP and Positioning

## Ideal Client Profile
Primary targets:
- Local service businesses doing $1M to $15M annual revenue
- Owner-led firms with 3 to 50 employees
- Already spending on marketing but inconsistent lead quality

Best verticals to start:
- HVAC, plumbing, roofing, legal, med spa, dental, home services

## Disqualifiers
- No clear offer
- No website control
- Pure startup with no operating cash
- Wants "viral" instead of measurable pipeline outcomes

## Core Pain Points
- "We get traffic but not enough qualified calls"
- "Competitors show up everywhere"
- "Our follow-up is slow or inconsistent"
- "We do not know what to fix first"

## Positioning Statement
We do not sell AI hype. We build a practical growth system that makes your business easier to find and easier to buy from.

## Messaging Angles
1. Revenue leakage: hidden losses in visibility and conversion
2. Speed: implement in days, not quarters
3. Clarity: one ranked action plan, no guesswork
4. Compounding: monthly gains through ongoing optimization

## Proof Elements to Collect Every Project
- Before/after metrics
- Fixed issues count
- New qualified leads or conversion lift
- Client quote tied to business result

## Fast Niche Offer Variants
- Home services: "Booked-call recovery and local AI visibility"
- Legal: "High-intent inquiry capture and authority visibility"
- Med spa: "High-value treatment funnel and visibility optimization"
