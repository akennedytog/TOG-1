# TheOneGroupAI - Business Building Documentation

**Started:** March 2026  
**Mission:** Build AI automation systems that actually work for SMBs  
**Approach:** Document everything, ship fast, learn in public

---

## What We're Building

An AI-powered automation system that:
- Handles repetitive business tasks without replacing humans
- Learns from feedback and improves over time
- Integrates with existing workflows (doesn't force new ones)
- Shows real ROI, not just "AI magic"

## Key Principles

1. **Build in public** - Share wins, losses, and lessons
2. **Ship fast, iterate faster** - Perfect is the enemy of shipped
3. **Measure everything** - If we can't measure it, we can't improve it
4. **AI augments, doesn't replace** - Humans stay in the loop
5. **Document for future us** - We will forget why we made decisions
