# South Florida Business Acquisition — Target Profile

**Prepared for:** Alec Kennedy | **Date:** August 30, 2026
**Purpose:** Define the ideal acquisition target for an SBA 7(a)-financed purchase of a cash-flowing business from a retiring ("boomer") owner.

---

## 1. The Opportunity

South Florida is a prime market for this play right now:
- A large wave of baby-boomer owners (est. 1946–1964) are retiring and selling.
- Many businesses are **owner-operated, low-tech, and cash-flowing** — exactly what SBA lenders like to finance.
- SBA acquisition lending hit **~$8.3B nationally across ~7,000 deals** in the most recent fiscal year (avg loan ~$1.2M).
- Miami alone saw **$45.9M in SBA acquisition loans across 37 deals** in 2025.

---

## 2. Target Financial Profile

| Metric | Sweet Spot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **SDE (Seller's Discretionary Earnings)** | **$150K – $300K** | Big enough to service debt + pay you; small enough to be affordable and owner-operated |
| **Asking price** | **$400K – $1.2M** | Fits your 10% down ($40K–$120K) with your $175K liquid |
| **Multiple (price ÷ SDE)** | **2.0x – 3.5x** | Below 2x = red flag; above 3.5x = overpriced for SBA |
| **Revenue** | $500K – $2M | Established, not a startup |
| **Years in operation** | **10+ years** | Boomer-owned, proven, financeable |
| **Owner transition** | Seller stays **6–12 months** | Protects cash flow during handover |

**Real South Florida examples (from current listings):**
- HVAC/Refrigeration contractor, Fort Lauderdale — $1.0M rev, **$624K SDE, 3.2x** (top-tier)
- Miami dessert brand — $1.3M rev, **$311K SDE**, $1.1M price
- Broward hair salon — **$116K SDE**, $210K price (smaller starter deal)
- Palm Beach pizzeria — 45 yrs old, $840K price

## 3. Target Industry Profile (best SBA fit)

**Ideal characteristics:**
- **Recurring revenue** (contracts, subscriptions, service agreements)
- **Low inventory / low capital intensity** (goodwill-heavy is fine — SBA lends on cash flow)
- **Not dependent on the owner's personal skill** (owner can step away)
- **Stable, boring, essential** (recession-resistant)

**Best-fit industries for South Florida boomer sellers:**
1. **HVAC / plumbing / electrical / pool service** — recurring service contracts, huge demand in FL
2. **Home inspection / property management** — turnkey, scalable, FL real estate boom
3. **Insurance agencies** — recurring premium income, top SBA-funded industry in Miami
4. **Landscaping / lawn care** — recurring, FL climate
5. **Cleaning / janitorial services** — recurring commercial contracts
6. **Auto repair / detailing** — cash-flowing, owner-operated
7. **Medical / dental / vet practices** — sticky clientele (higher multiple, but strong)
8. **Restaurants / food service** — only if strong SDE and real estate included (riskier)

**Avoid:** businesses dependent on the owner's personal relationships/skill, heavy inventory, or single large client.

## 4. Deal Structure (SBA 7(a))

For a **$1.0M** target acquisition:

| Component | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase price | $1,000,000 | |
| Working capital + fees | ~$100,000 | Roll into project cost |
| **Total project cost** | **$1,100,000** | |
| SBA 7(a) loan (90%) | $990,000 | |
| **Your cash down (10%)** | **$110,000** | At least **$55K must be your own unborrowed cash** (new Oct 1 rule) |
| Seller note (optional) | up to $55K | Only on full standby, max half the injection |

**SBA upfront guaranty fee:** ~2%–3.75% of guaranteed portion (on $1M loan ≈ **$30K–$37K**).

## 6. Recommended Search Parameters

- **Price range:** $400K – $1.2M
- **SDE range:** $150K – $300K
- **Multiple:** 2.0x – 3.5x
- **Industries:** service-based, recurring revenue (HVAC, insurance, home services, property management)
- **Owner transition:** 6–12 months seller stay
- **Location:** Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach (or anywhere in South Florida)
