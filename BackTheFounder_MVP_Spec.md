# "Back the Founder" — Social-First MVP Spec

**Status:** Concept → MVP spec (v1)
**Date:** 2026-08-20
**Author:** Alec Kennedy / The One Group
**Positioning:** Social platform to back high-potential founders. **MVP = social-only, NO securities.** Regulated investment layer (Reg CF) comes later, only after the social/backing demand is proven.

---

## 1. The Big Idea (one sentence)

A social feed where **vetted founders** share their current projects and **users back them** (from $5 up) — supporting the founder, spotting potential early, and tracking their journey. No equity, no securities at MVP. Just momentum, scorecards, and community.

### The long-term thesis (why this is worth building)
- Backing a **person** (not a company) is the highest-conviction form of early support — founders, not ideas, predict outcomes.
- Existing market signals confirm demand: Day1, Crowdsurf, Monisa, Athen are all building "invest in the individual." The wedge: we prove the **social/backing layer** first (fast, no compliance), then bolt on the Reg CF money layer.
- **MVP goal = validate the emotion:** "I spotted this founder early." If people return to watch their picks and back more, the regulated layer is worth building.

---

## 2. MVP Scope (what we build FIRST — keep it brutal)

**Principle:** Ship the smallest thing that proves the core loop. No equity, no trading, no wallet/blockchain, no funding-portal compliance in v1.

### In scope
| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| Founder onboarding + vetting | Trust layer = the moat |
| Founder profile with scorecard | The "product" users judge |
| Founder feed (updates) | Keeps the loop alive |
| "Back" a founder (impulse $5+) | The core action |
| Follow + update notifications | Return visits |
| Backer leaderboards + badges | Status + virality |
| Simple user profile ("X founders backed") | Identity / social proof |

### Out of scope (explicitly NOT v1)
- Real equity / securities / ISAs
- SEC funding portal registration
- Trading / resale of stakes
- Payment > simple card/tip processor
- Mobile native app (responsive web first)

---

## 3. Founder Side

### Vetting bar (the gate — this is your moat)
Founders must clear a bar to be listed. Start rigorous; loosen later:
- **Identity + background** verified (name, photo, LinkedIn/website, references)
- **Current project** with a real, shareable scope
- **Why they're worth backing** (compelling, specific — not "I want to be rich")
- **Commitment to post updates** (min cadence, e.g. weekly)
- **Consent to be public** (opt-in, full profile control, can go inactive)

### Scorecard (v1 fields — the credibility layer)
- Project name + one-line pitch
- Stage (idea / built / revenue / scaling)
- What they're building now
- Key milestones (with dates)
- Progress markers (updated by founder)
- Optional: track record / past work / social proof

### Founder behavior rules
- Post updates to their feed (milestones, wins, asks)
- Acknowledge/thank backers
- No securities talk; can't solicit "investment"

---

## 4. User (Backer) Side

### Core action: "Back a founder"
- One-click support from $5 up (impulse-friendly)
- Frames as **spotting potential early**, not donating
- Backer shows on the founder's page ("First to back," top backers)

### Why users come back
- **Follow** founders → update notifications
- **Track their picks** — "I backed this founder when they had 3 customers; now 300."
- **"Backed since" stamp** — the "bought early" feeling without any equity
- Leaderboards / badges ("Sharpest eye" when your backed founder hits a milestone)

### The NOT-money mechanics (critical)
These make it *feel* like investing without being one:
- Milestone tracking (watch picks progress)
- "Backed since $5" framing
- Founder gratitude (shoutouts, updates, small founder-branded rewards)
- Backer scorecard ("3 founders backed, 12 boosts, 1 milestone hit")

---

## 4. What "Success" Looks Like (MVP metrics)

Do NOT measure revenue first. Measure the **repeat-backing + return-visit** loop:

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| % users who back ≥2 different founders | Is backing a behavior, not a one-off? |
| % backers returning to check progress | Do people *care* about their founder's journey? |
| Milestones → more backs | Proves the feed loop compounds |
| Would a user tell a friend "I backed someone early"? | Virality — the whole model's engine |

**MVP kill/pivot test:** If people back once and never return, the "investing in humans" emotion isn't sticky enough → iterate on the tracking/scorecard or pivot. If they return and back more → bolt on the money layer.

---

## 5. Growth / Virality Loop (the engine)

- **"I backed them early"** = shareable identity. Every back is a potential social post.
- Founder milestone → notification → user shares ("my pick just hit X").
- Leaderboards make it competitive / bragging-rights.
- Referral mechanic: invite a friend, both get a small boost / badge.

---

## 6. Real Risks (MVP phase) + Mitigations

1. **Becomes a fan club without teeth** → keep "spotting potential + scorecard" framing sharp; it's about *potential*, not charity.
2. **Founder supply** → seed v1 with founders Alec already knows (network advantage); curate first ~100.
3. **"Boost" vs "invest" confusion** → crystal-clear language: support, NOT equity. Avoid tripping into securities / false-advertising even at the social layer. Run copy by a lawyer before launch.
4. **Scalability of vetting** → start strict + manual, automate only after the bar is proven.

## 7. Suggested Next Steps (after spec)

1. **Design the founder scorecard** (the fields that make a founder compelling/credible).
2. **Design the virality loop** in detail (share moments, referral, "spotted early" mechanics).
3. **Validate the wedge** with 5–10 founders you know + a landing-page waitlist (does anyone sign up?).
4. **If demand proves out →** plan the Reg CF money layer (registered funding portal, $5M cap, accredited/non-accredited limits, 1-yr lockup, Form C disclosures).

---

## 8. Regulatory Note (context, not MVP)

The money layer later runs on **Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF)**:
- Raise up to **$5M / 12 mo** through an **SEC-registered funding portal** or broker-dealer
- **General public CAN invest** (non-accredited) with income/net-worth-based caps
- **1-year resale lockup**; Form C filings; anti-fraud + bad-actor rules apply
- This is a regulated marketplace, not a casual app — the compliance lift is the real barrier, and why the social-only MVP comes first.
