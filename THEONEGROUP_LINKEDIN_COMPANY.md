**Primary Choice: Information Technology and Services**

- Matches what you actually do

- Common for B2B service companies

- Computer Software (if you want to emphasize product over services)
- Marketing and Advertising (if positioning content-heavy)
- Management Consulting (if emphasizing strategy/audit side)

**Recommendation:** Stick with **Information Technology and Services** — most accurate and flexible.

## STEALTH MODE RULES

### DO:
✅ Post exclusively from company page
✅ Use "The One Group" or "we" in content
✅ Respond to comments as The One Group
✅ Share industry insights, not personal stories
✅ Build email list (own the relationship)

### DON'T:
❌ Post about The One Group from personal profile
❌ Share "founder journey" content
❌ Connect personal milestones to company
❌ Use personal photos on company page
❌ Announce "I'm building this"
