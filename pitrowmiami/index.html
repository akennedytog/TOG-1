The Story

Pit Row Miami was born from a simple idea: bring the thrill of Formula 1 racing to any event in South Florida. No track required.

We invested in two brand new Fanatec F1 simulator rigs — the same equipment used by professional drivers — paired with 48" widescreens and the latest F1 26 game on PS5.

Every booking includes professional setup, staffed operation, and full teardown. We handle everything so you can focus on your guests.
