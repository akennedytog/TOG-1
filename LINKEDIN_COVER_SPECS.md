# LinkedIn Company Cover Image Specifications

## Dimensions
- **Recommended:** 1128 x 191 pixels
- **Minimum:** 1128 x 191 pixels
- **Maximum file size:** 8 MB
- **Format:** PNG or JPG

## Safe Zone
LinkedIn crops cover images differently on desktop vs mobile.

**Safe zone (text/logo visible on all devices):**
- Center 1000 x 191 pixels
- Keep important content in the middle 60%
- Avoid edges for text/logos

## Design Guidelines

### Layout Options

#### Option 1: Left-Right Split
```
[LOGO]                    [TAGLINE]
[Company Name]            [Value Prop]
                          [CTA]
```
- Logo on left
- Text on right
- Clean, professional

#### Option 2: Centered Focus
```

        [TAGLINE - Large]
        [Subtext - Smaller]
        
```
- Tagline centered
- Gradient background
- Minimal, bold

#### Option 3: Bottom Banner
```


[Full width gradient/image]
[TAGLINE in bottom third]
```
- Background image/gradient
- Text overlay at bottom
- Most common approach

## Color Scheme
Use your brand colors:
- Primary: Blue (#3b82f6)
- Accent: Purple (#8b5cf6)
- Dark: Navy (#0f172a)
- Gradient: Blue to Purple

## Text Content

**Tagline:**
"Automate the Boring. Scale the Important."

**Optional Subtext:**
"8-Agent AI System | 24/7 Automation"

**Optional CTA:**
"https://theonegroup.info"

## Fonts
- Headline: Inter Bold or Space Grotesk Bold
- Size: 24-32px (for 1128px width)
- Keep it readable at small sizes

## What NOT to Do
❌ Too much text (gets lost at small sizes)
❌ Logos at edges (get cropped)
❌ Busy backgrounds (distract from message)
❌ Small text (illegible on mobile)
❌ Stock photos of people (obvious, impersonal)

## Tools to Create
- Canva (linkedin banner template)
- Figma (design tool)
- Adobe Express (free)
- Photopea (free Photoshop alternative)

## Preview Check
After uploading, check on:
- Desktop browser
- Mobile app
- Tablet

Text should be readable on all three.
