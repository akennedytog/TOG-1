## 🥤 Breakfast — Smoothies (hide the foods you don't like)

### Berry Almond Oat Smoothie
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk (or 2% milk)
- 1/2 cup frozen mixed berries
- 1/4 cup rolled oats
- 1 tbsp almond butter (or 2 tbsp almonds)
- 1 scoop vanilla protein powder (~20g protein)
- 1/2 frozen banana (creaminess)
- Ice

Blend 30–45 sec. **~380 cal, ~30g protein** with powder.

**Swaps:**
- **Chocolate PB:** cocoa + peanut butter instead of berries
- **Green:** spinach + mango (can't taste the spinach)
- **Coffee:** cold brew + banana + oats (breakfast + caffeine)

# 💪 OPTIONAL DAY 5 — GLUTES + WAIST SHAPER (hourglass focus)

**PRIVATE note (not in the shared sheet):** this optional 5th day is designed to build the "Miami 10" physique — round, lifted glutes + a slim, defined waist for the hourglass figure. In the sheet it's titled neutrally as **"Day 5 - Glutes + Waist"**.

**How to use it:** run 1–2x/week (e.g. Saturday), or swap it in for an arm day if legs feel fresh. Glutes need recovery — don't run it more than 2x/week.

**Full session is in the Google Sheet tab "Day 5 - Glutes + Waist". Core structure:**

**Key principles are ALSO baked into the main days** (so the hourglass goal is hit weekly even without Day 5):
- **Day 1 + Day 3 core:** Stomach Vacuum added (the key waist-puller), Pallof Press added to Day 3 (Day 1 already had it + Side Plank + Dead Bug).
- **Overview Key Rule #8:** states the hourglass principle — upper-glute lifts round/lift the glutes, vacuum + anti-rotation slim the waist, avoid heavy side bends/obliques (they widen the waist), and arm/shoulder days widen the top for the taper.
- **Day 5 remains the optional concentrated version** for when she has time to really target glutes + waist.

**Stair stepper finisher (added to both arm days, Day 2 + Day 4):** 15-20 min glute-biased cardio. Cues: big steps, push through heels, lean back slightly, DON'T lean on rails — biases glutes over quads. Complements the hourglass goal (fat burn + glutes) on the shorter arm days.

**Glute rounders (the booty lifts):**
- Barbell Hip Thrust — 4 x 10-12 (the money lift for upper-glute "lift")
- Sumo Deadlift — 3 x 8-10 (glutes + inner thigh)
- Bulgarian Split Squat — 3 x 10-12/leg
- Cable/DB Glute Kickback — 3 x 12-15/side (isolation squeeze)
- Banded Glute Bridge burnout — 3 x 15-20 (band above knees)
- Cable Pull-Through (optional) — 3 x 12-15

**Waist-slimmers (hourglass — pull it in, don't widen it):**
- Stomach Vacuum — 3 x 15-20s hold (pulls waist in)
- Pallof Press — 3 x 12/side (anti-rotation, tightens waist)
- Dead Bug — 3 x 10/side
- Side Plank — 2 x 20-30s/side
- Kneeling Cable Crunch — 2 x 12-15

**Golden rule:** avoid heavy side bends / heavy oblique loading — that widens the waist. Use vacuum + anti-rotation moves to slim it.

**Why it works for hourglass:** upper-glute lifts lift + round the booty, vacuum/Pallof slim the waist, and the lean waist + round glutes + the existing arm/shoulder days (wider top) = the hourglass taper.

# 🔥 30-MINUTE RECIPE ROTATION (pick when you're not in the mood)

*All chicken/steak, 30 min max, minimal cleanup. Same portion-split rule applies: cook 1.5–2x protein, her half smaller on carbs than yours. Pick 1 per night — these rotate so you never get bored.*

## 1. 15-Minute Lemon Pepper Chicken Skillet
- 2 chicken breasts, butterflied thin (or 1 lb). Season lemon pepper + salt.
- Sear in olive oil over med-high 3–4 min/side (cook through ~5 min total if thin).
- Deglaze pan with 1/4 cup chicken broth + juice of 1 lemon + 2 tbsp butter. Simmer 1 min.
- Serve over quick-cook rice or with a microwaved sweet potato + steamed broccoli.
- **~25 min total.**

## 2. Steak Fajita Bowl (20 min)
- Slice 1 lb sirloin thin; toss with fajita seasoning.
- High heat skillet: sear steak 1–2 min/side, remove. Sauté peppers + onion in same pan 3 min.
- Serve over rice (or riced cauliflower for her) + sour cream + salsa.
- **~20 min.**

## 3. Chicken Stir-Fry (20 min, one pan)
- 1 lb chicken breast cubed; stir-fry in hot oil 4–5 min until cooked.
- Add bag of frozen stir-fry veg; cook 3–4 min.
- Sauce: 3 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp honey + 1 tsp garlic + 1 tsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water. Toss 1 min.
- Serve over rice (more for you, less for her).
- **~20 min.**

## 4. Air-Fryer (or Oven) Chicken Thighs + Veg (25 min)
- 1.5 lb chicken thighs, season salt/pepper/garlic/paprika.
- Air-fry at 400°F 18–20 min (or oven 425°F 25 min).
- Toss broccoli/asparagus in olive oil + salt, air-fry alongside 8–10 min.
- **~25 min, zero pans.**

## 5. Steak + Sheet-Pan Veggies (25 min)
- 1 lb steak (sirloin/flank), season salt/pepper/garlic.
- Broil or pan-sear 4–5 min/side (to medium), rest 5 min.
- Same time: sheet pan of halved Brussels sprouts + red onion + olive oil, roast 425°F 20 min.
- **~25 min.**

## 6. Chicken Alfredo-ish (20 min, no heavy cream)
- 1 lb chicken breast, bite pieces, sear in olive oil 4–5 min.
- Add 1 cup chicken broth + 1/2 cup Greek yogurt + 1/2 cup parmesan + garlic + salt/pepper. Simmer 3–4 min, stir until sauce thickens.
- Toss with zucchini noodles (for her) or whole-wheat pasta (for you).
- **~20 min.**

## 7. Ground Beef "Power Bowl" (15 min)
- 1 lb lean ground beef (or turkey), brown in skillet 5–6 min with taco seasoning.
- Build bowls: beef + rice (you: more) + black beans + avocado + salsa + Greek yogurt.
- **~15 min. Cheapest + fastest option.**

## 8. Chicken Tenders (oven) + Dipping Sauce (20 min)
- 1 lb chicken breast, cut into strips, toss in olive oil + paprika/garlic/salt.
- Bake 425°F 15–18 min on a sheet pan.
- Dip: 1/4 cup Greek yogurt + 1 tbsp hot sauce + 1 tsp honey.
- Serve with roasted or raw veg.
- **~20 min.**

## 9. Steak + Loaded Baked Sweet Potato (30 min)
- Microwave 2 sweet potatoes 6–8 min (poke first).
- Pan-sear 1 lb steak 4–5 min/side, rest 5 min, slice.
- Split potatoes, top with butter + cheese + steak + green onion.
- **~30 min.**

## 10. 20-Minute Chicken Quesadillas
- 1 lb chicken (leftover or quickly cooked), shred/chop.
- Fill tortillas with chicken + cheese, pan-toast 2 min/side until golden.
- Serve with salsa + Greek yogurt dip. Add a side salad for her.
- **~20 min.**
