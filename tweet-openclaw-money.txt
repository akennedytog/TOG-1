I see this question in OpenClaw comments daily:

"How can this make money?"

The answer? It doesn't.

OpenClaw isn't a money printer. It's a force multiplier.

It gives you the infrastructure to build faster, iterate quicker, and execute at scale. But you still need:
• A vision worth pursuing
• A market that actually wants it
• The guts to ship when it's imperfect

The tool doesn't create the business.
The human does.

Every successful OpenClaw user I've seen isn't asking "how do I monetize this?"

They're asking "how do I solve this expensive problem faster than everyone else?"

That's the frame shift.

Automation doesn't replace strategy.
It just removes the excuse that you "don't have time."

What's the expensive problem you're sitting on?
