## Industry-Specific Research Notes

### HVAC Industry Insights (South Florida)

**Peak Seasons:**
- May-September (AC season)
- Hurricane season (June-November) = emergency surge
- October maintenance rush before snowbirds return

**Typical Firm Profile:**
- 5-25 technicians
- Family-owned or small regional chains
- Emergency/after-hours = 40-60% of revenue
- Competition intense, reviews matter

**Decision Maker:**
- Usually owner or general manager
- Often answering phones themselves
- Pain point: growth outpacing infrastructure

---

### Legal Industry Insights (South Florida)

**High-Value Practice Areas:**
- Personal Injury (contingency, high case values)
- Family Law (emotionally urgent)
- Criminal Defense (time-sensitive)
- Immigration (large Spanish-speaking population)

**Intake Challenges:**
- 24/7 nature (arrests happen anytime)
- Need immediate empathy/professionalism
- Spanish bilingual requirement (Miami-Dade)
- Pre-qualification saves attorney time

**Decision Maker:**
- Managing partner or intake coordinator
- Often former paralegal promoted
- Pain point: volume vs. quality tension

---

### Accounting Industry Insights (South Florida)

**Seasonal Intensity:**
- January-April: Tax season (70% annual revenue)
- October: Extension deadline
- December: Year-end planning rush

**Client Expectations:**
- High-net-worth clients expect white-glove
- Quick response = retention
- Mistakes during crunch = referrals lost

**Decision Maker:**
- Managing partner or owner
- Often wearing too many hats
- Pain point: hiring seasonal help never works

## Compliance & Best Practices

### Email Compliance
- Include unsubscribe link in all emails
- Honor opt-outs within 24 hours
- Follow CAN-SPAM requirements
- Store proof of consent where applicable

### LinkedIn Limits
- Maximum 100 connection requests/day per account
- Space out connection requests
- Personalize every request (don't use generic)

### General Best Practices
- Research every prospect before outreach
- Reference specific, visible information
- Never use "Dear Sir/Madam" or generic openings
- Reply to responses within 2 hours
- Track everything in CRM/spreadsheet
