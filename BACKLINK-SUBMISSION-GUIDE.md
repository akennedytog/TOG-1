# 1. Yelp — Claim Business

- **Status:** FREE (claiming is free; advertising is optional/paid)
- **Exact URL:** https://biz.yelp.com/claim (or start at https://www.yelp.com/biz/start)
- **Required fields:** Business name (search or add), then after claiming: business category, address, phone, website, hours, photos, business description, service area.
- **Dofollow backlink:** **No** — Yelp links are nofollow. Still a high-value local citation for NAP consistency and local pack ranking.
- **Verification:** Phone call or postcard code.
