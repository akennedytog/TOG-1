## 🚀 Aggressive Growth Tactics

### 1. **Volume Strategy**
- Current: 3 posts/day
- **New target: 5 posts/day** (add 11 AM and 6 PM)
- More touchpoints = more algorithm exposure

### 2. **Thread Strategy**
- 1 thread/week on major topic
- 5-7 tweets per thread
- Build authority on single subject

### 3. **Engagement Loops**
- Reply to 5 relevant tweets/day
- Target: SMB owners, AI enthusiasts, business accounts
- Add value, don't promote

### 4. **Local Hooks**
- Reference South Florida constantly
- "Miami SMBs..." "West Palm..." "Fort Lauderdale..."
- Geographic specificity builds local following

### 5. **Service Teasers**
- "I helped a [industry] client [result]"
- Lead magnets in bio
- Clear CTA: "DM for AI audit"

```

ROI: [X months]

Want the same for your business?
DM me "audit" and I'll show you exactly where AI can help.

### Template B: The Contrarian
```
