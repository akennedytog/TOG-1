## ⚠️ Due Diligence Checklist (before any offer)

- [ ] Verify SDE (ask for 3 years tax returns + P&L)
- [ ] Confirm recurring revenue % (PM contracts, service agreements)
- [ ] Check customer concentration (top 5 customers % of revenue)
- [ ] Verify licenses transfer (HVAC, insurance, etc.)
- [ ] Confirm seller transition/training period (want 6-12 months)
- [ ] Check lease terms (if real estate not included)
- [ ] Run through deal modeler with real numbers
- [ ] Confirm SBA pre-qualification status
