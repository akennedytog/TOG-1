# Direct Web Scraping Lead Generation Strategy

## Why Skip LinkedIn?
- **Higher response rates** - Email is less crowded than LinkedIn
- **More direct** - Reach decision makers directly
- **Scalable** - No connection limits
- **Trackable** - Better analytics

## Lead Sources (No LinkedIn Required)

### 1. Google Maps API
- Search: "HVAC Miami", "Law firm Fort Lauderdale"
- Get: Business name, address, phone, website
- Volume: 100s per search

### 2. Yelp Business Directory
- Filter by city + category
- Get: Business info, reviews, contact details
- Volume: 50-100 per category

### 3. Chamber of Commerce Directories
- Miami-Dade Chamber
- Broward Chamber  
- Palm Beach Chamber
- Get: Member lists with contact info

### 4. Industry Association Websites
- FL BAR (lawyers)
- ACCA (HVAC contractors)
- Florida Realtors
- Get: Licensed professionals

### 5. Direct Website Scraping
- Crawl company websites
- Extract: Contact pages, email patterns, phone numbers
- Use: Hunter.io, Voila Norbert for email finding

### 6. State Business Database
- Sunbiz.org (Florida business registry)
- Get: All registered businesses
- Filter: By industry, location, size

## Automation Workflow

### Daily (Automated)
1. Scrape 50 new businesses from Google Maps
2. Extract contact info from websites
3. Enrich with Hunter.io/Apollo.io
4. Score and prioritize
5. Add to outreach queue

### Daily (Manual - 30 min)
1. Review top 10 leads
2. Personalize first sentence
3. Send emails (copy/paste)
4. Mark as contacted
5. Set follow-up dates

### Weekly (Automated)
1. Generate follow-up emails
2. Track response rates
3. Update pipeline
4. Send weekly report

## Success Metrics
- **Target:** 50 emails/day
- **Response rate:** 10-15%
- **Meeting rate:** 20% of responders
- **Close rate:** 25% of meetings
- **Time per close:** 2-3 weeks

## Tools Needed
- **Lead Gen:** Apify (Google Maps scraper)
- **Email Finding:** Hunter.io, Apollo.io
- **Email Sending:** Gmail + mail merge
- **Tracking:** Streak CRM or HubSpot
- **Automation:** OpenClaw (scripts)
