How It Works

1. Smart Receipt Capture

Technicians snap photos of receipts from anywhere. AI extracts vendor, amount, tax, and category. No manual data entry.

2. Automated Invoicing

Jobs marked complete in the field trigger automatic invoice generation and customer notification. Same-day billing.

3. AI Reconciliation

Bank transactions matched to receipts automatically. Discrepancies flagged for review. Monthly close in hours, not days.

Cash position, outstanding invoices, job profitability — all updated hourly. Make decisions with current data.
