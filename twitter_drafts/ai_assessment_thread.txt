TWITTER THREAD: The AI Assessment Business Model
=================================================

--- TWEET 1 (HOOK) ---
A guy offered me $1,000 to follow him around for a day and tell him where he's wasting time.

I said no.

Not because it wasn't interesting—but because it doesn't scale.

Here's what I built instead 👇

---

--- TWEET 2 (THE REALIZATION) ---
But his request revealed something massive: people don't need more AI tools.

They need someone to look at their chaos and tell them what to do.

The demand is there. The model just needed to change.

---

--- TWEET 3 (THE PIVOT) ---
So I productized it.

$999 AI Assessment. Flat rate. 1-week delivery.

Here's the exact process:

• 45-min recorded interview (I use @fathom_ai to transcribe)
• Drop the transcript into Claude with a custom analysis prompt
• Results formatted into a clean report using @Gamma

Takes me ~2 hours. Client gets a roadmap.

---

--- TWEET 4 (THE PROCESS) ---
The assessment covers:

→ Current workflow audit
→ AI opportunity mapping  
→ Tool recommendations with specific vendors
→ Implementation priority (what to do first, second, third)

No generic fluff. Every recommendation ties to their actual business.

---

--- TWEET 5 (THE UPSELL LADDER) ---
Here's where it gets interesting.

The $999 assessment isn't the endgame—it's the entry point.

After they see the roadmap, they hit you with:

"Can you just... do this for us?"

That's where the real money lives.

---

--- TWEET 6 (THE REAL NUMBERS) ---
Upsells I'm seeing:

• Custom GPT builds: $3K-5K
• Workflow automation: $5K-10K
• Team training + implementation: $5K-15K

The assessment is the thin edge of the wedge.

It builds trust fast and surfaces bigger projects.

---

--- TWEET 7 (REAL EXAMPLE) ---
Recent client: business broker drowning in 400 emails/day.

Assessment spotted the pattern: 80% were repetitive qualification questions.

Built him a custom GPT that drafts responses from his CRM data.

Project total: $3,800 ($999 + $2,801 implementation)

Took 6 hours of my time.

---

--- TWEET 8 (THE MINDSET SHIFT) ---
Stop selling "AI consulting."

Start selling clarity.

Business owners don't want to become prompt engineers.

They want someone to hand them a marked-up map and say "start here."

That's the product.

---

--- TWEET 9 (CTA) ---
If you're decent with AI and understand a specific industry:

This model is wide open.

No competition because most people overcomplicate it.

You're not selling complexity.
