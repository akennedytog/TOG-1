# TikTok Scripts for AI Agent Workshop

## Script 1: "The Hook" (15-30 sec)
---
**Hook:** "I built an AI system that finds me 20 leads every morning while I drink coffee"

**Body:** 
- Screen recording of Arlo running
- "This is Arlo. He researches South Florida businesses."
- "By 9 AM, I have 20 qualified leads with websites, emails, everything"
- "Then Dante writes my content. Iris sends my emails."
- "8 agents. One system. Zero manual work."

**CTA:** "Want to see how? I'm doing a free workshop next [DAY]. Link in bio"

---

## Best Practices:
- Post between 9-11 AM or 7-9 PM EST
- Use trending sounds (check TikTok Creative Center)
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Text overlay with key numbers
- Pin the workshop link comment
- Reply to EVERY comment in first hour
- Duets/Stitches with AI/business content
