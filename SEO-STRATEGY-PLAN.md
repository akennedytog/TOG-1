# SEO Content + Backlink Strategy Plan
**Prepared for:** The One Group (Alec Kennedy)
**Date:** August 2026
**Market:** South Florida (Miami / Fort Lauderdale / West Palm Beach)
**Sites:**
1. **theonegroup.info** — AI automation agency for SMBs (AI audits, optimization, coaching, competitor intel)
2. **pitrowmiami.com** — F1 simulator rental (corporate events, parties, team building)

**Current state:** Both sites have near-zero organic traffic. This plan targets low-competition long-tail keywords that are realistically rankable in 90 days, plus a local-first backlink and citation foundation.

---

## Executive Summary

- **Strategy:** Win the **local + long-tail** battle first, not the head terms. Ranking for "AI automation agency" or "racing simulator rental" nationally is out of reach in 90 days. Ranking for **"AI automation for Miami law firms"**, **"racing simulator rental for corporate events Fort Lauderdale"**, or **"F1 simulator bachelor party Miami"** is achievable with focused content + local authority.
- **Priority order:** (1) Local SEO foundation (GBP + citations) → (2) High-ROI long-tail content → (3) Local + niche backlinks → (4) Press/pitches.
- **Success metric (90 days):** Both sites above zero — 20–50 targeted keyword positions in top-50, 30+ quality backlinks each, GBP established and mapped, 5–10 citations each.

# C. LOCAL SEO

## C1. Google Business Profile (GBP) — Setup Checklist
*Do for BOTH sites. This is the single highest-ROI local action.*

1. **Create/claim** at google.com/business (both theonegroup.info and pitrowmiami.com).
2. **NAP consistency:** Use the SAME business name, address, phone (N.A.P.) everywhere — GBP, website footer, all citations. Pick ONE phone per site.
3. **Categories (critical):**
   - TOG: *Business management consultant*, *Information technology services*, *Marketing agency*, *Artificial intelligence company* (secondary).
   - Pit Row: *Entertainment agency*, *Party equipment rental*, *Event planner*, *Amusement center* (secondary).
4. **Service areas** (if no physical storefront — common for both): Add Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Hollywood, Doral, Coral Gables, Boca Raton, Kendall.
5. **Full profile:** Description (with primary keyword), hours, website, phone, attributes (LGBTQ+ friendly, etc.), products/services list.
6. **Photos:** 10+ high-quality (office/team for TOG; simulators/events for Pit Row). Add new photos monthly.
7. **Posts:** 1–2/week (offers, blog links, event recaps).
8. **Q&A:** Seed 5+ questions/answers (e.g., "What areas do you serve?", "Do you provide setup?").
9. **Reviews:** Send a link to every satisfied client; respond to every review within 48h. Goal: 10+ reviews in 90 days.
10. **Verify** via postcard/phone/video per Google instructions. Add owner role for Alec; enable booking links if available.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Local first, long-tail second.** Chambers + GBP + Yelp/Bing/Apple citations are the fastest path from zero.
2. **Niche verticals win.** "AI for real estate/law/restaurants Miami" and "sim rental for bachelor/corporate/kids parties" are winnable; national head terms are not (yet).
3. **Backlink relevance > authority.** A Miami Chamber link outranks a generic national link for local searches.
4. **90-day goal is realistic:** above-zero traffic, 20–50 keyword positions, 30+ quality backlinks each, GBP + 10–15 citations each.
